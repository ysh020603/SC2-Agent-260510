## Summary

- Check if opponent has committed to anti-air units such as Mutalisk or Queen.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing air-oriented macro posture, increase economy and air strength, maintain defense and expansion, continue production and technology.

Human trajectory evidence indicates a heavy economy and technology posture with air-oriented army. Supported relations show Phoenix and Stalker counter Mutalisk, Zealot counters Zergling and Queen, Stalker counters Roach, but Mutalisk and Queen counter Oracle and VoidRay, and Roach counters Zealot and Stalker. This suggests a mixed composition is needed to handle ground and air threats.
