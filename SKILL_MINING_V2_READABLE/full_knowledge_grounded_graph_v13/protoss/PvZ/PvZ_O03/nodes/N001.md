# N001 — Technology/Economy Overextension with Inadequate Army

## Node Type

NEGATIVE

## Summary

Early game with heavy economy and technology investment, but army composition is unknown and production is only moderate. Opponent shows a ground posture with possible pressure. Avoid continuing to invest in technology or economy without simultaneously increasing army production and composition. Do not neglect defense or scouting.

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Queen. - Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

The trajectory shows a heavy technology/economy investment but only moderate production, leaving you vulnerable to early pressure. The opponent's ground posture and possible pressure require a stronger ground army to defend and transition safely.

## Applicability Checks

- Check if your army composition is unknown or weak relative to your economy and technology.
- Check if your production is insufficient to support your technology and economy investments.
- Check if the opponent is showing a ground posture with possible pressure.

## General Failure Mode

resource_to_army_conversion_failure

## Risk Direction

Historical matched contexts associate this broad direction with worse outcomes; the evidence is associative, not causal.

Avoid continuing to invest in technology or economy without simultaneously increasing army production and composition. Do not neglect defense or scouting.

## Safer Re-evaluation

Recheck when you have a clear ground army composition and production capacity that can handle potential pressure, or when you have confirmed the opponent's actual composition and intentions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced ground army with sufficient production to support your technology and economy advantages.
