## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late midgame with heavy economy and technology investment on both sides; opponent shows a ground-heavy, macro-oriented posture with possible pressure. Continue developing technology and economy while strengthening ground army; maintain defense and expansion; keep air presence moderate.

- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

Trajectory cues include Zealot, Stalker, Immortal, Sentry, DarkTemplar, Observer, WarpPrism, and Gateway, indicating a ground-heavy, tech-heavy composition with robotics support.

## Applicability Checks

- Check that your economy is heavy enough to support continued tech and production.
- Check that your technology buildings (e.g., TwilightCouncil, DarkShrine, RoboticsFacility) are completed or under construction.
- Check that your army composition includes a mix of gateway and robotics units to handle ground threats.
- Check that you have detection (Observer) and mobility (WarpPrism) available.
- Check that your expansion count is sufficient for late-midgame economy.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing technology and economy while strengthening ground army; maintain defense and expansion; keep air presence moderate.

Human trajectories show a heavy economy and technology posture with ground army strengthening. Supported relations indicate DarkTemplar can counter Zerg ground units, while Immortal is strong vs Roach; however, Hydralisk and Roach can counter several Protoss ground units, so maintain scouting and adapt.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain a flexible ground-based army with heavy tech and economy, ready to transition to air or additional tech if opponent shows air or spellcasters.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If resources are not being converted into army or tech effectively, recheck production queues, supply, and tech progression. If repeated failed posture persists, adjust composition based on live scouting.
