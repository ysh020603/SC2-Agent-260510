# N001 — Resource-to-army conversion failure in a heavy-economy ground-macro posture

## Node Type

NEGATIVE

## Summary

The node is triggered when the player has committed to a heavy-economy, technology-heavy ground posture but fails to convert accumulated resources into a sufficient army, leading to defeat. Avoid over-investing in technology and economy without corresponding production capacity. Do not neglect army composition and unit production while pursuing upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown. The opponent has a heavy economy and expansion posture, with possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy and technology investment is high, but production is only moderate and your army is ground-leaning. You have invested in upgrades and tech structures (e.g., Forge, Twilight Council, Robotics Facility) and are producing Zealots, Stalkers, and possibly Immortals. However, the failure mode indicates that resources are not being converted into army strength effectively.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

The trajectory includes actions like Probe, Pylon, Stalker, WarpGateResearch, Zealot, ProtossGroundWeaponsLevel1, Gateway, Charge, Immortal, ProtossGroundArmorsLevel1. This indicates a tech-heavy ground build with upgrades, but the failure suggests that the army size was insufficient relative to the economy.

## Applicability Checks

- Check if you have more than 500 unspent minerals or gas for more than 30 seconds.
- Check if your production structures are idle or not producing units.
- Check if your army supply is below 50% of your total supply capacity.
- Check if you have invested in multiple tech structures (e.g., Forge, Twilight Council, Robotics Facility) but have fewer than 3 production structures.

## General Failure Mode

resource_to_army_conversion_failure

## Risk Direction

Historical matched contexts associate this broad direction with worse outcomes; the evidence is associative, not causal.

Avoid over-investing in technology and economy without corresponding production capacity. Do not neglect army composition and unit production while pursuing upgrades.

## Safer Re-evaluation

If you detect resource_to_army_conversion_failure, recheck your production structures and spending. Build additional Gateways or Robotics Facilities if needed, and ensure you are constantly producing units. If the issue persists, consider reducing technology investment temporarily to free up resources for army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a state where production capacity matches economic output, ensuring a steady flow of units to defend and apply pressure.

## Knowledge-Grounded Execution Envelope

**Human-trajectory candidate pool (not an ordered build list):** Zealot, Stalker, Immortal

- Selection: Choose only a currently reachable candidate after checking live producers, prerequisites, resources, supply, and active queues; this is a candidate pool, not an ordered build list.
- Resource conversion: When the combined bank is at least 750 and army supply is still below 15 after 05:00, prefer a currently executable combat candidate from this pool before optional greed or deeper technology.
- Unreachable-candidate fallback: If the preferred candidate is not reachable before the next decision, use a cheaper currently producible trajectory candidate; do not queue a long blocked prerequisite chain while army supply is low.
- Feedback repair: If the bank/low-army deficit persists for two decisions, stop repeating the same plan, re-read the best matching node, and choose a different reachable candidate or producer bottleneck repair.
