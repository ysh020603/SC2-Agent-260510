# N010 — Midgame Ground-Macro Posture with Heavy Tech and Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

This node is triggered when the game reaches midgame with both sides exhibiting a ground-oriented, macro-heavy posture. The opponent shows moderate production and technology with possible pressure, while your own forces are heavily invested in ground production and technology. The strategic goal is to stabilize and develop further, increasing economy, production, and technology while maintaining defense and expansion. Strengthen ground army, increase economy, production, and technology, maintain defense and expansion, and stabilize tempo before further development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears moderate, and exact hidden counts remain unknown. The opponent's economy and expansion are heavy, and pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. You have access to Gateway, RoboticsFacility, Stargate, and units like Zealot, Stalker, Immortal, and Sentry. Your economy and expansion are heavy, and your defense is heavy. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

The trajectory shows a balanced development of economy, production, and technology with a ground focus. The presence of Gateway, RoboticsFacility, and Stargate indicates a versatile tech base. The units Zealot, Stalker, Immortal, and Sentry provide a solid core that can be adapted. The opponent's Zergling and Queen cues suggest a standard Zerg ground macro, which your Zealots can counter effectively.

## Applicability Checks

- Verify that your economy is heavy and you have sufficient resources to support increased production and technology.
- Confirm that your production structures (Gateway, RoboticsFacility, Stargate) are operational and not supply-blocked.
- Check that your army composition includes a mix of Zealots, Stalkers, and support units like Sentry or Immortal to handle ground threats.
- Ensure that your defense is sufficient to hold against possible Zergling pressure or Queen-based harassment.
- Monitor the opponent's tech and production to detect any hidden air or tech switch.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase economy, production, and technology, maintain defense and expansion, and stabilize tempo before further development.

The trajectory evidence indicates a heavy economy and technology investment on both sides. Your ground army can be strengthened by leveraging Zealot's effectiveness against Zerglings and Queens, and by using Sentry to synergize with Immortal. Increasing production and technology will support a robust midgame transition. The opponent's ground posture suggests focusing on ground counters and maintaining a strong defensive position.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a stronger ground army composition with heavy tech support, possibly including Robotics and Stargate units, while maintaining a solid economy and expansion lead.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If you notice a failure mode such as resource float or supply block, recheck your production and economy. If the opponent's composition changes, re-evaluate your unit choices. If you are repeatedly failing to convert resources into army, adjust your production priorities. If the game state becomes unstable, focus on stabilizing your economy and defense.
