# N004 — Midgame Ground Macro Posture with Heavy Tech and Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Entered when both sides are in midgame with heavy economy, production, and technology investment, and ground-oriented armies. Enemy air presence is light or uncertain, and no special threats are identified. Continue developing a technology/economy/production posture while strengthening ground army and maintaining defense. Increase economy, maintain expansion and air direction, and continue production and technology.

- Verify that your ground army composition is balanced and can handle the opponent's ground units (e.g., Zealots for Zerglings, Stalkers for Overseers, DarkTemplar for Queen/Zergling).

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain a strong ground army with heavy tech and economy, while preparing to adapt to any confirmed air threat or special threats. Continue to increase economy and production capacity.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If any failure mode is detected, re-evaluate the current posture. For resource-to-army conversion failure, focus on spending resources efficiently. For repeated failed postures, adjust strategy based on feedback. For composition mismatch, adapt unit composition. For runtime instability, ensure system stability. For supply blocks, manage supply proactively.
