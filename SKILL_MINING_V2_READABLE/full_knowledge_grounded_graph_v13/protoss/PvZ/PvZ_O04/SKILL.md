## Decision Nodes

### [NEGATIVE] N001 — Resource-to-army conversion failure in a heavy-economy ground-macro posture

**Trigger situation:**  
The node is triggered when the player has committed to a heavy-economy, technology-heavy ground posture but fails to convert accumulated resources into a sufficient army, leading to defeat.

**Risk direction:**  
Avoid over-investing in technology and economy without corresponding production capacity. Do not neglect army composition and unit production while pursuing upgrades.

**Read for details:** `N001`
