# N004 — Midgame Ground Macro Posture with Heavy Tech and Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Entered when both sides are in midgame with heavy ground-oriented macro, heavy production, and heavy technology investment; enemy air presence is light or uncertain. Strengthen ground army while continuing production and technology development; maintain defense and expansion; increase economy; maintain air presence.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Queen, Overseer. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

Trajectory cues (Zealot, Stalker, Immortal, Sentry, Observer, WarpPrism, DarkTemplar) indicate a ground-heavy army with tech support and detection, consistent with a defensive macro posture.

## Applicability Checks

- Check that enemy air presence is indeed light or uncertain before committing to ground-heavy composition.
- Verify that your economy and production can support continued tech investment without supply blocks.
- Confirm that you have detection (Observer) or a plan to get it before relying on DarkTemplar.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army while continuing production and technology development; maintain defense and expansion; increase economy; maintain air presence.

Human trajectories show a balanced macro posture with heavy tech and defense, aiming to develop safely while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition toward a stronger midgame army composition that can respond to enemy tech choices, while maintaining economy and defense.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If resource-to-army conversion failure is detected (high unspent resources with low army value), re-evaluate production and tech priorities. If repeated failed posture occurs, change strategic direction based on live scouting.

## Knowledge-Grounded Execution Envelope

**Human-trajectory candidate pool (not an ordered build list):** Stalker, Oracle, Zealot, Immortal, Sentry, Observer, WarpPrism, DarkTemplar

- Selection: Choose only a currently reachable candidate after checking live producers, prerequisites, resources, supply, and active queues; this is a candidate pool, not an ordered build list.
- Resource conversion: When the combined bank is at least 750 and army supply is still below 15 after 05:00, prefer a currently executable combat candidate from this pool before optional greed or deeper technology.
- Unreachable-candidate fallback: If the preferred candidate is not reachable before the next decision, use a cheaper currently producible trajectory candidate; do not queue a long blocked prerequisite chain while army supply is low.
- Feedback repair: If the bank/low-army deficit persists for two decisions, stop repeating the same plan, re-read the best matching node, and choose a different reachable candidate or producer bottleneck repair.
