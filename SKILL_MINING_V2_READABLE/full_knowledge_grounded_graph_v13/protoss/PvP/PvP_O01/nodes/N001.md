# N001 — PvP Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech/Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase where both sides show a ground-oriented macro posture with heavy production and technology investment, and possible pressure. Strengthen ground army, increase economy and technology, maintain defense and expansion, continue production, with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

Human trajectories in this node frequently include building Assimilator, Pylon, Gateway, Stalker, Immortal, RoboticsFacility, TwilightCouncil, and PhotonCannon, indicating a tech-heavy ground composition with defensive structures.

## Applicability Checks

- Check if you have at least one Gateway and a CyberneticsCore to support tech buildings.
- Check if you have sufficient gas income to support tech units like Stalker and Immortal.
- Check if your expansion is safe or if you need defensive structures like PhotonCannon.
- Check if your opponent has shown any air units or tech that would require a response.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase economy and technology, maintain defense and expansion, continue production, with safety checks.

Both sides are in a heavy macro ground posture; investing in economy and tech while maintaining a solid ground core allows flexibility to adapt to opponent's next move.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stronger ground army composition with tech upgrades while maintaining a healthy economy and scouting information.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If you are banking resources without spending them, or if repeated attempts to push fail, re-evaluate your army composition and tech choices based on scouting.
