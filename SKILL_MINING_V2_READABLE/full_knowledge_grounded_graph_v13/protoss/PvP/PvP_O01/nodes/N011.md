# N011 — Midgame Air-Macro Posture vs Ground-Macro Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame with own heavy air-oriented posture and opponent ground-macro posture, both with heavy economy and production. Continue developing air strength while maintaining economy and defense, with safety checks against potential ground pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Stalker, Sentry, Immortal. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Own army is air-oriented with heavy production and technology investment. Representative units include Stalker and Carrier. Economy and expansion are heavy. Defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

Trajectory cues include Probe, VoidRay, Pylon, Phoenix, Stalker, Oracle, Assimilator, PhotonCannon, Gateway, Zealot, indicating a mix of air and ground development. The policy signature suggests increasing air and technology while maintaining economy.

## Applicability Checks

- Check own current air unit composition and production capacity.
- Check opponent's visible ground composition and potential tech transitions.
- Check economy and expansion status to support continued production.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing air strength while maintaining economy and defense, with safety checks against potential ground pressure.

Human trajectory evidence shows a strong tendency to increase air direction and strengthen air army. Knowledge relations indicate that Stalker counters Carrier, VoidRay, Oracle, Phoenix, and Sentry, while Zealot counters Immortal and Stalker. Carrier synergizes with VoidRay, Phoenix synergizes with Oracle, Stalker, and VoidRay. These relations qualify the need for ground support and caution against pure air.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain a balanced air-ground composition, leveraging synergies while mitigating counters.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If resource-to-army conversion failure is detected, recheck production and spending efficiency. If feedback is not changing repeated failed posture, re-evaluate strategic direction based on live observations.
