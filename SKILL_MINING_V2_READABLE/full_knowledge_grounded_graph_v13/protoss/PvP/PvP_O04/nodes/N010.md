# N010 — Late Midgame Ground Macro vs Air Macro

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late midgame phase with own ground-oriented heavy economy/tech posture and opponent air-heavy posture. Strengthen ground army while increasing defense and technology, maintain economy and expansion, continue production, maintain air direction and upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an air posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Stalker, Carrier, VoidRay, Oracle. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and technology investment, heavy economy and defense, moderate air presence. Representative units include Stalker, Sentry, VoidRay, Observer.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

Human trajectory shows a macro-oriented ground posture with heavy tech and economy, consistent with a defensive, technology-driven approach.

## Applicability Checks

- Check own economy and production capacity to support continued ground army and tech investment.
- Check opponent air presence and composition to validate the need for anti-air ground units.
- Check own detection and vision coverage to support ground army movements.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army while increasing defense and technology, maintain economy and expansion, continue production, maintain air direction and upgrades.

Trajectory evidence indicates a ground-leaning macro posture with heavy tech and economy. Knowledge supports Stalker as a counter to air units like Carrier, Oracle, and VoidRay, and Observer synergizes with ground forces for detection and vision.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition toward a stronger ground army composition with supporting detection and anti-air capabilities.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

Recheck resource allocation and army composition if repeated failures occur; adjust production and tech priorities based on live feedback.
