# N010 — Ground Macro Posture with Heavy Production and Technology

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame phase where both sides exhibit a ground-oriented macro posture with heavy production and technology investment, and possible pressure. Strengthen ground army, increase economy, production, technology, and upgrades while maintaining defense and expansion. Maintain air presence as uncertain.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot, Stalker, Immortal. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

Trajectory cues include Stalker, Pylon, Probe, WarpGateResearch, Zealot, Gateway, ProtossGroundWeaponsLevel1, Charge, RoboticsFacility, Immortal, indicating a ground-heavy tech and production path.

## Applicability Checks

- Check that your economy can support heavy production and technology investments.
- Verify that your production structures and tech buildings are sufficient for the intended ground army.
- Confirm that enemy intelligence still indicates a ground posture with heavy production and technology.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase economy, production, technology, and upgrades while maintaining defense and expansion. Maintain air presence as uncertain.

Human trajectory evidence indicates a ground-macro posture with heavy production and technology. Knowledge relations support ground unit synergies and counters: Zealot counters Stalker and Immortal, Immortal counters Stalker, and Zealot synergizes with Immortal. Upgrades and Charge enhance ground units. WarpGateResearch enables Gateway morph.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Develop a technology/economy/production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

Recheck resource-to-army conversion and adjust production/technology investments. If repeated failed posture, reassess strategy based on live observations.

- Selection: Choose only a currently reachable candidate after checking live producers, prerequisites, resources, supply, and active queues; this is a candidate pool, not an ordered build list.
