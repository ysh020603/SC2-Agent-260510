# N002 — Maintain Flexible Technology/Economy/Production Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late midgame with unknown opponent posture and light/uncertain production, technology, and economy on both sides. No reliable combat-unit cues observed. Maintain current army path, economy, expansion, production, technology, and upgrade directions while developing with safety checks. Keep air direction maintained.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include no reliably grounded combat-unit cue. Production appears light/uncertain, technology investment appears light/uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture is broadly unknown-oriented with light/uncertain production and technology investment. Economy and expansion are light/uncertain. Army composition and style are unknown. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

Human trajectories at this node show a balanced approach with no strong preference for aggression or economy, supporting a maintain-and-develop strategy.

## Applicability Checks

- Check that no reliable enemy combat-unit cue has been observed.
- Check that own production and technology are still light/uncertain.
- Check that the game phase is late midgame.
- Check that no critical resource or supply deficit exists.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path, economy, expansion, production, technology, and upgrade directions while developing with safety checks. Keep air direction maintained.

With both sides showing light/uncertain production and technology, committing to a specific aggressive or greedy path risks being countered by an unknown opponent posture. Maintaining flexibility allows adaptation to live observations.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined posture once opponent intentions become clearer through scouting or engagement.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If resource-to-army conversion failure is detected, re-evaluate production and tech choices. If repeated failed posture persists, seek new information or adjust strategy.
