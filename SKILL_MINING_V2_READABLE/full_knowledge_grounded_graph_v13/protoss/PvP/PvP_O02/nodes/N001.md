# N001 — Early Game Technology/Economy/Production Development with Flexibility

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At the start of a PvP match, the opening is a technology/economy/production template. No reliable combat-unit cues are observed yet. The agent should develop a balanced posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation. Maintain current development path for economy, production, technology, and defense, while keeping army path flexible. Tempo is develop_with_safety_checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include no reliably grounded combat-unit cue. Production appears light_or_uncertain, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with light_or_uncertain production and light_or_uncertain technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

Human trajectories show a high frequency of this default node (support 522), indicating that early game often proceeds without immediate conflict. The policy signature is to maintain all directions, consistent with a flexible opening.

## Applicability Checks

- Check that no reliable enemy combat-unit cues have been observed.
- Verify that your own production and technology are still light_or_uncertain.
- Confirm that the game phase is early_game.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path for economy, production, technology, and defense, while keeping army path flexible. Tempo is develop_with_safety_checks.

The opening is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Early game information is sparse, so committing to a specific aggressive or greedy posture is premature. Maintaining a balanced development posture allows adaptation based on live observations.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more specific posture once enemy intentions become clearer, while ensuring economy and production are not disrupted.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If resource_to_army_conversion_failure is detected, re-evaluate production and army composition. If feedback_not_changing_repeated_failed_posture, reassess strategic direction based on new intelligence.
