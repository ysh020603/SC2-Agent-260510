# PvT_O03 Technology / Economy / Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvT_O03
- Matchup: Protoss vs Terran
- Opening Family: technology / economy / production opening
- Method: Knowledge-Grounded Full V13

## Opening Strategy

This opening emphasizes a heavy technology and economy focus with moderate production, aiming to establish a strong foundation while remaining adaptable to opponent actions. The army composition is ground-leaning, and the strategy relies on scouting and flexibility rather than a fixed build order.

Develop a technology / economy / production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Technology/Economy/Production Development with Ground Posture

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology investment on both sides; opponent shows ground-leaning posture with possible Marine and Hellion cues.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing technology, economy, and production while strengthening ground army and maintaining defense. Increase economy, production, and technology; maintain expansion and defense.

**Read for details:** `N001`

### [NEGATIVE] N002 — Resource-to-army conversion failure with heavy economy and technology investment

**Trigger situation:**  
Heavy economy and technology posture with moderate production, but army strength is insufficient relative to investment; repeated failed postures without feedback-driven adjustment.

**Risk direction:**  
Avoid continuing to invest in economy and technology without a corresponding increase in army production; avoid ignoring repeated failed postures.
