# N002 — Resource-to-army conversion failure with heavy economy and technology investment

## Node Type

NEGATIVE

## Summary

Heavy economy and technology posture with moderate production, but army strength is insufficient relative to investment; repeated failed postures without feedback-driven adjustment. Avoid continuing to invest in economy and technology without a corresponding increase in army production; avoid ignoring repeated failed postures.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Reaper. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

The trajectory shows a heavy economy and technology focus with moderate production, but the negative node indicates that this posture has historically been associated with defeat when army conversion fails.

## Applicability Checks

- Verify that your current economy and technology investments are not leaving you with insufficient army to defend against early pressure.
- Check that your production facilities are actively producing units and not idle.
- Confirm that your army composition can handle the opponent's observed ground forces, including Reapers.

## General Failure Mode

Resource-to-army conversion failure

## Risk Direction

Historical matched contexts associate this broad direction with worse outcomes; the evidence is associative, not causal.

Avoid continuing to invest in economy and technology without a corresponding increase in army production; avoid ignoring repeated failed postures.

## Safer Re-evaluation

If you detect that your army is too small relative to your economy and technology, shift production towards combat units and consider delaying further tech or expansions until you have a sufficient defensive force.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced posture where army strength matches your economy and technology investment, while maintaining flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.
