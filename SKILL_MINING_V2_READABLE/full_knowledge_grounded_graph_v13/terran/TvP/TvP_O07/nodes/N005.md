# N005 — Midgame Ground Macro Posture with Heavy Economy and Technology

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

This node is triggered when both you and the opponent are in a midgame phase with heavy economy, heavy production, heavy technology, and a ground-leaning army posture. Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with possible Zealot and VoidRay cues, but air presence remains light or uncertain. Strengthen ground forces while maintaining defense and production. Continue technology development and increase economy. Maintain expansion and air direction. Develop with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot and VoidRay. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown. Defense and expansion are heavy, and pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Representative units include SiegeTank, Marine, Hellion, and Medivac. Economy and expansion are heavy, and defense is heavy. Air presence is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

The trajectory shows a focus on ground units (Marine, Marauder, SiegeTank, Hellion) and infrastructure (SupplyDepot, Refinery, Barracks, Factory). The presence of Medivac and WidowMine suggests a mixed composition with support and defensive elements. The heavy economy and technology investment indicate a macro-oriented strategy.

## Applicability Checks

- Check that your current army composition is ground-leaning and includes sufficient anti-air capability to handle possible VoidRay threats.
- Verify that your economy and production are heavy enough to support continued unit production and technology upgrades.
- Confirm that your defensive posture is adequate to withstand potential pressure from the opponent.
- Ensure that your technology development is progressing as planned and that you have the necessary infrastructure (e.g., Armory, Starport) for required upgrades and units.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces while maintaining defense and production. Continue technology development and increase economy. Maintain expansion and air direction. Develop with safety checks.

The trajectory evidence indicates a balanced macro posture where both sides are investing heavily in economy and technology. The ground-leaning army composition suggests that reinforcing ground capabilities and maintaining a strong defensive posture is appropriate. The presence of possible VoidRay cues warrants maintaining some anti-air capability, but the overall focus remains on ground strength.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a stronger ground army composition with supporting technology upgrades, while maintaining a solid economy and defensive structure. Be prepared to adapt if the opponent reveals a different composition.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If a failure mode is detected, re-evaluate the current posture and adapt based on live observation. For resource-to-army conversion failure, check that resources are being spent efficiently. For feedback not changing repeated failed posture, consider altering the strategic direction. For knowledge-visible composition mismatch, verify that the observed enemy composition matches the assumed ground posture. For runtime or API instability, ensure system stability. For live supply block with bank, adjust supply depot construction timing.
