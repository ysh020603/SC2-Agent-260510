# N002 — TvP O07 Economy / Technology / Expansion Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

This node is triggered when the game reaches the midgame phase with a heavy economy and technology posture, and both sides are primarily ground-based. The opponent's air presence is light or uncertain, and the own army is ground-oriented with moderate air presence. Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain your expansion and defense posture, and increase your air presence. The tempo is to develop with safety checks.

Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown. The opponent's economy and expansion posture are heavy, with moderate defense and possible pressure.

You have a heavy defense posture and are capable of applying pressure. Representative units include SiegeTank, Marine, Reaper, and Hellion.

- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

- Check that your economy is indeed heavy and you have sufficient income to support continued production and tech.
- Verify that your opponent's army is still primarily ground-based and that no significant air threat has been detected.
- Ensure that your production facilities are active and you are not supply blocked.
- Confirm that your technology upgrades are progressing as planned.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain your expansion and defense posture, and increase your air presence. The tempo is to develop with safety checks.

The trajectory evidence indicates a heavy economy and technology investment, with a ground-oriented army. The opponent is also ground-oriented with heavy production and technology. To maintain an advantage, you should continue to grow your economy and tech, while ensuring your ground army is strong enough to defend and potentially pressure. Increasing air presence can provide flexibility against possible tech switches.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a stronger midgame army composition that can either defend against a potential push or apply pressure, while continuing to expand and tech up. Aim to have a solid ground core with some air support.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If you notice that your resources are accumulating without being spent on army or infrastructure, or if you are repeatedly failing to defend against pushes despite a strong economy, recheck your production and army composition. Also, if the opponent reveals a tech switch or a significant air presence, re-evaluate your own tech and air investments.
