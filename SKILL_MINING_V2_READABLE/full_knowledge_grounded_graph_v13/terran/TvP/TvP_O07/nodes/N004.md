# N004 — Late Midgame Ground Macro Posture with Heavy Economy and Technology

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

This node is triggered when both you and the opponent are in a late midgame phase with heavy economy, heavy production, and heavy technology investment, and the opponent is observed or remembered to be in a ground posture with representative units such as Stalker, Sentry, Observer, and Immortal. Strengthen your ground army while continuing production and technology development. Increase defense and economy, maintain expansion and air presence, and stabilize before developing further.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Stalker, Sentry, Observer, Immortal. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown. Air presence is light or uncertain, and special threats are not identified.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. You have representative units such as SiegeTank, Marine, and Medivac, and you have access to structures like Barracks, Factory, Starport, EngineeringBay, and MissileTurret. Your economy and expansion are heavy, and your defense posture is heavy. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

The trajectory shows a focus on building a solid ground-based army with SiegeTank, Marine, and Medivac, while also investing in defensive structures like Bunker and MissileTurret. The inclusion of Marauder and Starport indicates a commitment to a bio-mech composition. The heavy economy and technology investment suggest a long-term plan to outproduce and out-tech the opponent.

## Applicability Checks

- Check that the opponent's army is primarily ground-based with no significant air presence.
- Verify that your own production and technology are sufficient to support a ground army.
- Ensure that your economy can sustain continued production and expansion.
- Confirm that you have the necessary structures (Barracks, Factory, Starport) and upgrades to produce the desired units.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army while continuing production and technology development. Increase defense and economy, maintain expansion and air presence, and stabilize before developing further.

The opponent's ground posture with heavy technology and production suggests a potential for strong ground engagements. Your heavy economy and technology allow you to invest in a robust ground force composition that can counter the opponent's likely units. The presence of SiegeTank and Marauder provides anti-armor capabilities against Stalker and Immortal, while Marine and Medivac offer a solid bio core with healing and mobility. Maintaining a defensive posture with Bunker and MissileTurret helps secure your bases against possible pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger ground army composition with SiegeTank, Marauder, Marine, and Medivac, supported by upgrades and defensive structures. Continue expanding and increasing production capacity to support a sustained midgame push or to transition to late-game units if needed.

If you experience supply blocks or resource conversion issues, focus on macro management. If runtime or API instability occurs, ensure your decision-making process remains robust.
