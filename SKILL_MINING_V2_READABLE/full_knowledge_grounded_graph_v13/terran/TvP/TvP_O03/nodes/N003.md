## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

The trajectory shows a typical macro opening: SCV production, supply depots, refineries, and tech structures (Factory, Starport) leading to SiegeTank and Marine production, with a Bunker for defense. This aligns with a ground-heavy, tech-heavy posture.

## Applicability Checks

- Check that your economy is heavy and you have sufficient resources to support continued production and tech.
- Check that your production structures (Barracks, Factory, Starport) are active and not idle.
- Check that your army composition includes a mix of Marines and SiegeTanks to counter ground threats.
- Check that your expansion is secure and defended, possibly with a Bunker.
- Check that you have not neglected scouting; maintain awareness of opponent's tech and army composition.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing technology and production while strengthening ground forces and increasing economy, maintaining defense and expansion posture with safety checks.

The trajectory evidence indicates a balanced macro-oriented approach: heavy economy and technology support sustained ground production. The presence of SiegeTank and Marine in the trajectory suggests a composition that can counter Stalker-based ground armies, while the heavy economy allows for continued expansion and tech progression.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If resources are not being converted into army or tech, recheck production queues and worker allocation. If repeated failed postures occur, re-evaluate opponent composition and adjust tech choices accordingly.
