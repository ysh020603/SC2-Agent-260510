## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

The trajectory shows a heavy economy and production with a mix of ground and air units, including Marine, Hellion, Banshee, and Battlecruiser. The presence of Banshee and Battlecruiser indicates an air transition, which aligns with the policy signature to strengthen air. The opponent's ground posture with Colossus suggests that air units can bypass the ground threat.

## Applicability Checks

- Verify that the opponent's army is indeed ground-leaning with no significant air presence.
- Confirm that your economy can support the cost of transitioning to air units like Battlecruiser.
- Ensure that your production facilities (Starport with TechLab and FusionCore) are available or being built.
- Check that your current army composition is not overly vulnerable to the opponent's ground forces while the air transition is underway.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen air capabilities while continuing to develop economy, production, and technology. Maintain defensive readiness and expand further. The tempo is to develop with safety checks.

Human trajectory evidence indicates a direction toward increasing air presence and strengthening air forces, while continuing economy and expansion. The opponent's ground-heavy posture with Colossus and other ground units can be addressed by air units like Banshee and Battlecruiser, which are effective against ground targets. However, the knowledge base also shows that Colossus counters many ground units, so transitioning to air provides a strategic advantage.

## Transition Goal

Transition toward a stronger air component, potentially including Battlecruisers, while maintaining a solid ground backbone and economy. Continue expanding and upgrading.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If the opponent is confirmed to have a significant air presence, re-evaluate the air transition. If resource-to-army conversion is failing, recheck production and spending. If the ground posture is repeatedly failing, consider a faster transition to air or a different composition.
