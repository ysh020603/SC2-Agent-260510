# N003 — Expansion / Economy / Technology Ground Macro Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy and technology investment, ground-oriented army, and possible pressure from opponent. Continue developing economy and technology while strengthening ground forces and maintaining defense. Increase air capability as a hedge, but do not overcommit.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Stalker. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

Human trajectories frequently involve SCV, Marine, SupplyDepot, a currently reachable knowledge-verified option, Medivac, Marauder, SiegeTank, Refinery, WidowMine, and Barracks, indicating a standard macro-oriented ground army with tech and economy focus.

## Applicability Checks

- Check current supply and production capacity to ensure no supply block.
- Verify that key structures (Barracks, Factory, Starport) are operational or under construction.
- Confirm that economy is heavy and expansion is underway or planned.
- Assess opponent's visible units and tech to confirm ground posture and no immediate air threat.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing economy and technology while strengthening ground forces and maintaining defense. Increase air capability as a hedge, but do not overcommit.

Human trajectories show a heavy economy and technology posture with ground army development. Supported relations indicate that Marauder, Reaper, and SiegeTank counter Stalker, while Stalker counters Marauder, Medivac, Reaper, and WidowMine. Synergies among Marine, Marauder, Medivac, and SiegeTank support a robust ground composition. Maintain flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition toward a stronger mid-game army with Medivac support and SiegeTank or WidowMine presence, while continuing to expand and tech.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If any failure mode is detected, re-evaluate the situation: check resource allocation, adjust composition based on observed enemy, ensure production is not idle, and resolve supply blocks promptly.
