## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In late midgame, own ground macro posture with heavy production and tech, opponent shows air-heavy posture with heavy economy and tech. Strengthen ground army while increasing air presence and technology; maintain defense and expansion; continue production.

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence consistent with air posture; representative cues include Zealot, Stalker, Sentry, Phoenix. Production and technology appear heavy, exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Own ground-oriented macro posture with heavy production and technology investment, heavy economy and defense. Representative units include Marine, Reaper, Medivac, WidowMine.

- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army while increasing air presence and technology; maintain defense and expansion; continue production.

Trajectory evidence indicates a ground-leaning army with heavy tech; increasing air presence and technology supports flexibility against air-heavy opponent while maintaining strong ground core.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

Develop a balanced army with increased air capability while preserving heavy economy and tech.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If resources accumulate without army growth, recheck production and tech; if repeated failed posture, adjust composition based on live scouting.
