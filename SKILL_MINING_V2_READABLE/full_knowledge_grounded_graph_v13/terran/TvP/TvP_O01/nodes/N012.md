# N012 — Ground Macro Posture with Heavy Tech and Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy and production, ground-oriented army, and heavy technology investment. Opponent shows air-macro posture with moderate air presence and heavy tech/production. Strengthen ground army while maintaining economy, production, and technology development. Continue expanding and defending. Maintain air direction as is.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an air posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Stalker, VoidRay. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

Human trajectory shows a ground-oriented macro build with heavy tech and economy, consistent with the node's posture.

## Applicability Checks

- Check if you have sufficient anti-air capabilities (e.g., Marines, WidowMines) to handle VoidRay pressure.
- Verify that your economy can sustain continued production and tech upgrades.
- Confirm that your ground army composition includes units that counter Stalker (e.g., Marauder, SiegeTank).

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army while maintaining economy, production, and technology development. Continue expanding and defending. Maintain air direction as is.

Ground forces like Marauder and SiegeTank are effective against Stalker, and Marine can counter VoidRay with sufficient numbers. Heavy economy supports continued production and tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Develop a robust ground army with supporting tech and economy, ready to adapt to opponent's air threats.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If army composition is mismatched with opponent's visible forces, re-evaluate unit choices. If economy is not converting to army, adjust production and tech priorities.

- Selection: Choose only a currently reachable candidate after checking live producers, prerequisites, resources, supply, and active queues; this is a candidate pool, not an ordered build list.
- Resource conversion: When the combined bank is at least 750 and army supply is still below 15 after 05:00, prefer a currently executable combat candidate from this pool before optional greed or deeper technology.
- Unreachable-candidate fallback: If the preferred candidate is not reachable before the next decision, use a cheaper currently producible trajectory candidate; do not queue a long blocked prerequisite chain while army supply is low.
- Feedback repair: If the bank/low-army deficit persists for two decisions, stop repeating the same plan, re-read the best matching node, and choose a different reachable candidate or producer bottleneck repair.
