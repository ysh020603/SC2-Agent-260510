# N009 — Technology/Economy/Production Opening vs Air-Macro Protoss

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame default node: both sides heavy economy and production, opponent shows air-macro posture with Stalker/VoidRay cues, own ground-macro posture with Marine/SiegeTank cues. Strengthen ground army while increasing defense and economy; maintain expansion and air direction; continue production and technology; stabilize then develop.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an air posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Stalker, VoidRay. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown. Expansion and economy are heavy; defense is moderate.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented macro posture with heavy production and technology investment. Representative units include Marine and SiegeTank. Defense and economy are heavy; expansion is heavy. Air presence is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

Human trajectories frequently built MissileTurret, Bunker, Marine, SiegeTank, Marauder, and Starport, indicating a defensive ground-oriented response to an air-heavy opponent.

## Applicability Checks

- Check live enemy composition for confirmed air units (e.g., VoidRay) and ground support.
- Check own production buildings and tech labs to ensure Marauder/SiegeTank availability.
- Check economy and supply to sustain continued production and expansion.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army while increasing defense and economy; maintain expansion and air direction; continue production and technology; stabilize then develop.

Trajectory evidence shows a ground-heavy Terran posture with defensive structures (Bunker, MissileTurret) and tech/economy investments (Refinery, Starport). Supported relations indicate Marauder and SiegeTank counter Stalker, and Marine counters VoidRay, so reinforcing ground forces aligns with the observed enemy air-macro posture.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition toward a robust ground army with sufficient anti-air support, while maintaining economy and tech lead.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If army composition fails to counter observed enemy units, re-evaluate production and tech choices; if repeated failures occur, adjust strategy based on live scouting.
