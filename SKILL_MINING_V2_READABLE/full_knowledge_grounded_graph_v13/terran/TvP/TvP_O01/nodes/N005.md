# N005 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro Posture with Heavy Economy and Technology

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

This node is triggered when the agent is in the early-midgame phase, with a ground-oriented army, heavy economy, heavy production, and heavy technology investment, while the opponent shows a similar ground macro posture with heavy economy and technology, and light or uncertain air presence. Strengthen ground army, increase economy, maintain production and technology development, with safety checks for potential pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot, Stalker, Observer. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

The trajectory shows a focus on ground units (Marine, Marauder, SiegeTank) and infrastructure (SupplyDepot, Refinery), with occasional harassment units (Reaper, Hellion, WidowMine). This supports a ground macro posture with heavy economy and tech.

## Applicability Checks

- Check that the opponent's air presence is light or uncertain; if significant air is detected, this node may not apply.
- Verify that your own army composition is ground-leaning and that you have sufficient production and tech structures.
- Ensure that your economy is heavy enough to support continued production and tech investments.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase economy, maintain production and technology development, with safety checks for potential pressure.

The trajectory evidence indicates a heavy economy and technology posture for both sides, with a ground-leaning army. The supported relations show that your ground units (e.g., Marauder, SiegeTank, Hellion) have tactical advantages against the opponent's likely ground units (Zealot, Stalker). Therefore, continuing to develop ground forces and economy aligns with the observed statistical direction.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger midgame army composition that leverages ground advantages while maintaining a healthy economy and tech lead.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If the agent detects that it is not converting resources into army effectively, recheck production queues and ensure that army composition matches the opponent's threats. Also recheck for supply blocks and idle production structures.

## Knowledge-Grounded Execution Envelope

**Human-trajectory candidate pool (not an ordered build list):** SiegeTank, Marine, Marauder, WidowMine, Reaper, Hellion

- Selection: Choose only a currently reachable candidate after checking live producers, prerequisites, resources, supply, and active queues; this is a candidate pool, not an ordered build list.
- Resource conversion: When the combined bank is at least 750 and army supply is still below 15 after 05:00, prefer a currently executable combat candidate from this pool before optional greed or deeper technology.
- Unreachable-candidate fallback: If the preferred candidate is not reachable before the next decision, use a cheaper currently producible trajectory candidate; do not queue a long blocked prerequisite chain while army supply is low.
- Feedback repair: If the bank/low-army deficit persists for two decisions, stop repeating the same plan, re-read the best matching node, and choose a different reachable candidate or producer bottleneck repair.
