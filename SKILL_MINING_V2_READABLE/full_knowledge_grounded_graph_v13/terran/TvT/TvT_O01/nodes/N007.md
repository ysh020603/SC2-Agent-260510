## Summary

This node is triggered when both players are in the late midgame phase, with heavy economy, production, and technology investment, and a ground-oriented army composition. The opponent is observed to have a ground posture with representative cues such as SiegeTank and Marauder, while your own posture includes SiegeTank, Marine, Hellion, and Medivac. Strengthen ground army, increase defense, economy, and expansion, continue production and technology, maintain air presence, and develop with safety checks.
