# N002 — Ground Macro Posture with Heavy Economy and Technology

## Node Type

NEGATIVE

## Summary

Early-midgame phase where both players exhibit heavy economy, production, and technology investment, with ground-oriented armies and light or uncertain air presence. Avoid neglecting army production in favor of pure economy or technology. Avoid over-committing to air without confirmed enemy air presence.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include no reliably grounded combat-unit cue. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Representative units include Reaper. Defense posture is light or uncertain. Economy and expansion are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

The trajectory shows a heavy economy/tech opening with ground units like Reaper, Marine, SiegeTank, and Hellion. The negative node indicates that this posture is vulnerable if not properly converted into army strength.

## Applicability Checks

- Check that your economy is indeed heavy and you have sufficient production structures.
- Verify that your technology investments are aligned with your intended army composition.
- Confirm that your army composition is not severely mismatched against known enemy units.

## General Failure Mode

resource_to_army_conversion_failure

## Risk Direction

Historical matched contexts associate this broad direction with worse outcomes; the evidence is associative, not causal.

Avoid neglecting army production in favor of pure economy or technology. Avoid over-committing to air without confirmed enemy air presence.

## Safer Re-evaluation

If you notice that you are banking resources without producing units, or if repeated failed postures occur, re-evaluate your production and army composition. Also recheck if live supply blocks occur with a large bank.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
