# N006 — Heavy Economy and Technology Development with Ground Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame state where both players have heavy economy and technology, with ground-oriented armies and possible pressure. Continue developing economy and technology while strengthening ground defense and maintaining flexibility for adaptation.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with possible Marine and Reaper presence. Their production and technology appear heavy, but exact counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and technology investment. You have defensive structures and units such as SiegeTank and Marine, and are expanding and teching up.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

The trajectory indicates a focus on economy and technology with defensive measures, consistent with a macro-oriented approach in a mirror matchup.

## Applicability Checks

- Check that you have sufficient economy to support continued production and technology.
- Verify that your defensive structures are positioned to protect key expansions.
- Confirm that your unit composition can handle potential ground threats.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing economy and technology while strengthening ground defense and maintaining flexibility for adaptation.

The trajectory shows a balanced development of economy, technology, and defense, which is appropriate when the opponent's exact composition is uncertain but likely ground-based.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stronger midgame position with a solid economy, key technologies, and a defensive setup that can respond to opponent moves.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If you are floating resources without army production, or if scouting reveals a different opponent posture, adjust your build accordingly.

## Knowledge-Grounded Execution Envelope

**Human-trajectory candidate pool (not an ordered build list):** SiegeTank, Marine

- Selection: Choose only a currently reachable candidate after checking live producers, prerequisites, resources, supply, and active queues; this is a candidate pool, not an ordered build list.
- Resource conversion: When the combined bank is at least 750 and army supply is still below 15 after 05:00, prefer a currently executable combat candidate from this pool before optional greed or deeper technology.
- Unreachable-candidate fallback: If the preferred candidate is not reachable before the next decision, use a cheaper currently producible trajectory candidate; do not queue a long blocked prerequisite chain while army supply is low.
- Feedback repair: If the bank/low-army deficit persists for two decisions, stop repeating the same plan, re-read the best matching node, and choose a different reachable candidate or producer bottleneck repair.
