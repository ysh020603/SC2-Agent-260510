# N003 — Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech and Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology investment on both sides; ground-oriented army posture with light or uncertain air presence. Strengthen ground forces, increase defense and economy, continue production and technology development, maintain air presence, and develop with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include SiegeTank, Marine, Marauder. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

Human trajectories show a focus on economy and technology with defensive structures like Bunker and MissileTurret, and production of units like Marine, Hellion, and SiegeTank.

## Applicability Checks

- Check if you have sufficient economy to support continued production and technology upgrades.
- Check if your defensive structures are adequate against potential ground pushes.
- Check if your army composition is effective against the opponent's observed units.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces, increase defense and economy, continue production and technology development, maintain air presence, and develop with safety checks.

Both sides are investing heavily in economy and technology, with a ground-focused army. Maintaining a strong defensive posture while continuing to grow economy and tech allows for flexibility to respond to opponent's moves.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a stronger ground army composition with sufficient defensive structures and continued economic growth.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If you are failing to convert resources into army strength or if repeated failed postures are not being corrected, re-evaluate your production and army composition.
