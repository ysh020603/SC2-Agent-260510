Strengthen ground forces, increase economy, continue technology development, maintain defense and expansion, stabilize tempo before further development.

Human trajectories show a focus on ground units (Marine, Reaper) and infrastructure (Barracks, Factory, Starport, SupplyDepot, Refinery) with heavy economy and technology. The presence of SiegeTank suggests a transition toward a more defensive, positional army.

## Applicability Checks

- Verify that your current economy and production are sufficient to support continued unit production and technology upgrades.
- Confirm that your army composition is predominantly ground-based and that you have not committed heavily to air units.
- Check that your opponent's observed units and structures are consistent with a ground macro posture; if air presence becomes confirmed, reassess.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces, increase economy, continue technology development, maintain defense and expansion, stabilize tempo before further development.

Both sides are investing heavily in economy and technology, so a stable ground macro posture allows you to leverage your production and tech advantages while avoiding risky commitments. The presence of SiegeTank and Marine synergies supports a strong defensive and positional play.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
