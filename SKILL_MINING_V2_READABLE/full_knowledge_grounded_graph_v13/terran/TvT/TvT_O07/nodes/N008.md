# N008 — Technology/Economy/Production Development with Ground-Macro Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase where both sides show heavy economy, production, and technology investment, with ground-oriented armies and possible pressure. Continue developing technology, economy, and production while strengthening air capabilities. Maintain defense and increase expansions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Marine, Reaper. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and technology investment. Representative units include SiegeTank, Marine, and Medivac. Economy and expansion are heavy. Air presence is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

The trajectory shows a balanced development of economy, production, and technology with a ground army. The presence of air units in cues suggests a gradual shift towards air superiority.

## Applicability Checks

- Verify own economy and production are on par with heavy posture.
- Confirm opponent ground posture through scouting (e.g., Marine, Reaper).
- Check if own technology is sufficient for desired air units.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing technology, economy, and production while strengthening air capabilities. Maintain defense and increase expansions.

Human trajectory evidence indicates a heavy economy and technology posture with a ground army. The presence of Medivac and SiegeTank suggests a marine-tank composition, while Banshee and Battlecruiser cues indicate potential air transition. Strengthening air provides flexibility against possible opponent air or ground pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a more air-capable army while maintaining a strong ground core, leveraging SiegeTank and Marine with Medivac support.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If resources are banked without army production, recheck production structures and resource allocation. If repeated failed engagements occur, reassess composition and technology choices.
