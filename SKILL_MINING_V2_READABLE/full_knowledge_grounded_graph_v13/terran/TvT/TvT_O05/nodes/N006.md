Continue developing technology, economy, and production while strengthening ground forces and increasing defense; maintain air presence.

The trajectory shows a balanced heavy economy and technology investment with ground army focus. Knowledge supports SiegeTank as a strong counter to Marine and Reaper, and Marine synergizes with SiegeTank. Banshee is a potential air threat that can counter Marine and SiegeTank, so maintaining detection and anti-air is prudent.
