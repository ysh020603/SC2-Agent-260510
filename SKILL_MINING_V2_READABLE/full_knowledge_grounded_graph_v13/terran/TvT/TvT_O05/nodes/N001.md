# N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro Posture with Heavy Economy and Technology

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with both sides showing heavy economy, heavy technology, and ground-oriented armies. Production is moderate for opponent and heavy for self. Air presence is light or uncertain. Pressure is possible but not confirmed. Strengthen ground army, increase economy and production, continue technology development, maintain expansion and defense, stabilize tempo before developing further.
