## Opening Strategy

This opening emphasizes a balanced development of technology, economy, and production infrastructure, aiming to establish a strong macro foundation while remaining adaptable to opponent actions. The strategy leans towards a ground-based army composition with a heavy economic focus, allowing for flexible transitions based on scouting information.

Develop a technology / economy / production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: heavy
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning
