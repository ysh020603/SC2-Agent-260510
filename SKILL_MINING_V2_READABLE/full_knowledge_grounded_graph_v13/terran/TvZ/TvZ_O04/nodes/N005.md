# N005 — Late Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech and Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Continue developing your technology and economy while strengthening your ground army. Maintain defense and increase air capabilities as a hedge against possible tech switches.

Continue developing your technology and economy while strengthening your ground army. Maintain defense and increase air capabilities as a hedge against possible tech switches.

The trajectory evidence indicates a heavy economy and production posture for both sides. Your ground forces have strong counter relationships against the opponent's observed ground units, but the opponent's heavy production and possible pressure require continued defensive investment. Increasing air presence provides flexibility against potential tech switches.
