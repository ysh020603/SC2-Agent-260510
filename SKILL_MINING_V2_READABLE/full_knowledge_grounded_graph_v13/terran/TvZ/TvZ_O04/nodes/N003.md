## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

Historical association is not causality. The trajectory shows a midgame ground macro posture with heavy production and technology. Supported relations indicate tactical interactions between your units and likely enemy units, but these do not dictate a preferred action.

- Verify that your current economy and production are sufficient to support continued technology investment.
- Confirm that enemy air presence remains light or uncertain before committing to ground-only focus.
- Check that your army composition includes enough anti-ground capability to handle possible Zergling/Roach pressure.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army while increasing economy and continuing technology development; maintain defense, production, and expansion; keep air presence at current level.

Human trajectory evidence indicates a heavy economy and technology posture with ground army strengthening. Supported relations show tactical advantages for your ground units against likely enemy ground units, but also some enemy advantages against your units, so maintain safety checks.
