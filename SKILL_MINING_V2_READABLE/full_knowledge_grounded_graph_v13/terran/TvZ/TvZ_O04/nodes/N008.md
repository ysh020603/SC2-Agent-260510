# N008 — Technology/Economy/Production Opening with Ground Macro Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, both sides ground-leaning with heavy economy and production; opponent technology light or uncertain, own technology heavy. Continue developing technology and production while increasing economy; maintain ground army strength and defense; adapt based on live observation.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; no reliable combat-unit cue observed. Production moderate, technology light or uncertain, economy and expansion heavy, defense heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and technology investment. Representative units include Marine and Reaper. Economy and expansion are heavy, defense light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

Human trajectories show a heavy economy and production opening with ground units, consistent with a technology/economy/production posture. The presence of Marine, Reaper, Factory, Starport, and SiegeTank cues supports a flexible ground army.

## Applicability Checks

- Check own economy: ensure sufficient mineral and gas income to support heavy production and tech.
- Check production structures: verify Barracks, Factory, Starport are active and producing.
- Check army composition: confirm ground units (Marine, Reaper, Hellion, SiegeTank) are being produced.
- Check opponent scouting: maintain awareness of opponent's tech and air transitions.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing technology and production while increasing economy; maintain ground army strength and defense; adapt based on live observation.

The trajectory shows a technology/economy/production opening with ground units. Supported synergies (Marine-SiegeTank, Reaper-Marine, SiegeTank-Reaper) indicate a flexible ground composition that can handle various threats while maintaining economic growth.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a mid-game composition that leverages ground synergies while maintaining tech and economy; be ready to adapt to opponent's tech or air transitions.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If army composition mismatches visible enemy cues or if repeated failed postures occur, re-evaluate production and tech choices; ensure resources are being converted into army effectively.

## Knowledge-Grounded Execution Envelope

**Human-trajectory candidate pool (not an ordered build list):** Marine, Reaper, Hellion, SiegeTank

- Selection: Choose only a currently reachable candidate after checking live producers, prerequisites, resources, supply, and active queues; this is a candidate pool, not an ordered build list.
- Resource conversion: When the combined bank is at least 750 and army supply is still below 15 after 05:00, prefer a currently executable combat candidate from this pool before optional greed or deeper technology.
- Unreachable-candidate fallback: If the preferred candidate is not reachable before the next decision, use a cheaper currently producible trajectory candidate; do not queue a long blocked prerequisite chain while army supply is low.
