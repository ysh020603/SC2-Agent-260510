- Opponent ground posture, heavy economy, moderate production, light or uncertain technology, possible pressure, representative Queen cues.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

- Own economy moderate, technology heavy, production moderate, expansion light or uncertain, defense light or uncertain, army unknown.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.
