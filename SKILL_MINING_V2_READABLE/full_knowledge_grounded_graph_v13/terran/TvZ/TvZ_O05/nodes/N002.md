# N002 — Midgame Ground-Macro Development with Heavy Economy and Technology

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

This node applies when the game reaches midgame with both sides exhibiting heavy economy and technology investment, while the opponent shows a ground-oriented army posture with possible pressure. The own posture is ground-leaning with moderate air presence and heavy production. Continue developing the ground army while increasing economy, expansions, and technology. Maintain defense and consider increasing air presence. The tempo is to develop with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Hydralisk, Roach, Queen. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

The trajectory shows a focus on economy and technology with ground forces, including SiegeTank, Reaper, Hellion, and Banshee. The presence of Battlecruiser suggests a potential air transition. The opponent's ground units (Zergling, Hydralisk, Roach, Queen) are countered by the own units according to the knowledge base.

## Applicability Checks

- Verify that the opponent's army is indeed ground-oriented and not transitioning to air.
- Confirm that the own economy and technology are sufficient to support continued production and upgrades.
- Check that the current army composition can handle the opponent's observed units.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing the ground army while increasing economy, expansions, and technology. Maintain defense and consider increasing air presence. The tempo is to develop with safety checks.

The trajectory evidence indicates a heavy economy and technology posture for both sides. The opponent's ground-oriented army can be addressed with the existing ground forces, while the heavy economy supports further expansion and tech. The moderate own air presence provides flexibility to counter potential air threats or harass.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a stronger midgame army composition that can apply pressure or defend against opponent's heavy ground forces, while continuing to expand and tech up.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If resource-to-army conversion failure is detected, re-evaluate production and spending. If feedback is not changing a repeated failed posture, reassess strategic direction. If composition mismatch is visible, adjust army composition. If runtime instability occurs, stabilize the environment. If supply block with bank, build supply depots.
