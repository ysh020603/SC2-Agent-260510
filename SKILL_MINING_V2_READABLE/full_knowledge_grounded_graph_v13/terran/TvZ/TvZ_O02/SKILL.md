## Opening Strategy

A flexible opening that develops technology, economy, and production infrastructure while remaining adaptable to opponent actions. The approach emphasizes a light or uncertain early army composition, prioritizing strategic options over committing to a fixed build order.

Develop a technology / economy / production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

- Economy: light_or_uncertain
- Production: light_or_uncertain
