# N003 — Maintain Flexible Technology/Economy/Production Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame with light or uncertain production, technology, and economy; no reliable combat-unit cues from the opponent. Maintain current army path and continue developing technology, economy, and production while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include no reliably grounded combat-unit cue. Production appears light_or_uncertain, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture is broadly unknown-oriented with light or uncertain production and technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

Human trajectories at this node show a balanced approach with no strong preference for aggression or economy. The sign is default, indicating that neither a positive nor negative label is warranted; the node is a flexible holding pattern.

## Applicability Checks

- Check that your current production and technology structures are consistent with a light_or_uncertain posture.
- Verify that you have not already committed to a specific army composition or tech path.
- Confirm that the opponent has not shown a clear pressure or expansion posture that would force a response.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path and continue developing technology, economy, and production while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

The opening template emphasizes flexibility and adaptation. With no strong signals of opponent pressure or composition, committing to a specific response would be premature. Maintaining a balanced posture allows you to react to the first reliable enemy cue without overcommitting.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a state where you have sufficient information to choose a concrete strategic direction, either by scouting or by forcing a reaction from the opponent.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If you notice that your resources are banking up without a corresponding increase in army or infrastructure, re-evaluate your production and tech choices. Also recheck if you are repeatedly failing to respond to the opponent's posture changes.
