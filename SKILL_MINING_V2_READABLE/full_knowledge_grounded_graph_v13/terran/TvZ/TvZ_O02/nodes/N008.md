# N008 — Ground Macro Development with Technology Investment

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase where both sides show a ground-oriented posture; opponent has heavy economy and possible pressure, while own technology investment is heavy. Strengthen ground forces while continuing technology development and increasing economy, with safety checks against possible pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light or uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented army with moderate production and economy, heavy technology investment. Representative units include Marine. Defense posture is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

Human trajectories show a ground macro posture with moderate production and heavy technology investment. The presence of Hellion, SiegeTank, Marauder, and WidowMine suggests a tech-heavy ground army.

## Applicability Checks

- Check if own economy can support continued technology investment.
- Check if opponent has committed to a ground-heavy composition.
- Check if own production facilities are sufficient for desired army composition.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces while continuing technology development and increasing economy, with safety checks against possible pressure.

Human trajectories show a ground macro posture with technology investment. Supported relations indicate that Hellion, Marine, and Reaper can counter Zergling, while Zergling counters Marine and Marauder. Synergies among Marine, Marauder, SiegeTank, WidowMine, and Reaper support a robust ground composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain a flexible ground composition that can transition to heavier tech or production as needed, while expanding economy.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If resource-to-army conversion failure is detected, recheck economy and production balance; if feedback not changing repeated failed posture, reassess strategic direction.
