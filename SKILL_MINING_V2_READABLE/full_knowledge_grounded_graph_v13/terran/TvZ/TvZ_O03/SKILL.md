## Opening Strategy

A strategic template emphasizing technology, economy, and production development, with a ground-leaning army composition and heavy economic investment. The opening is designed to maintain flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

Develop a technology / economy / production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

- Production: moderate
