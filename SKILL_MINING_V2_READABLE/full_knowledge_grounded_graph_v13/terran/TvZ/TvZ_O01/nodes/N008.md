# N008 — Midgame Ground Macro Posture with Heavy Economy and Technology

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

This node is triggered when the game reaches midgame with both sides exhibiting a ground-oriented, macro-focused posture. The opponent shows heavy economy, expansion, and defense, with moderate production and possible pressure. Your own posture is similarly ground-oriented with heavy production and technology, and you are in a position to develop further while maintaining safety. Strengthen ground forces, increase economy and technology, maintain defense and expansion, and continue air development. Develop with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Hydralisk, Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

The trajectory shows a focus on economy and technology with a ground army. The presence of SCV, Marine, SupplyDepot, a currently reachable knowledge-verified option, Hellion, Refinery, SiegeTank, Medivac, EngineeringBay, and Marauder indicates a standard Terran macro opening. The opponent's cues (Zergling, Hydralisk, Queen) suggest a Zerg ground-based composition. The policy signature aligns with this: strengthen ground, increase economy and technology, maintain defense and expansion.

## Applicability Checks

- Check that the game is in midgame phase and both sides have a ground-oriented posture.
- Verify that the opponent's observed units are consistent with Zergling, Hydralisk, Queen.
- Ensure that your own production and technology are heavy, and economy is heavy.
- Confirm that you have the necessary infrastructure (Barracks, Factory, Starport, EngineeringBay) to produce the intended units.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces, increase economy and technology, maintain defense and expansion, and continue air development. Develop with safety checks.

The trajectory evidence indicates a heavy economy and technology investment on both sides, with a ground-oriented army composition. The policy signature suggests increasing economy and technology while maintaining defense and expansion. The supported relations show that your ground units (Marine, Hellion, SiegeTank) have counter relationships against the opponent's likely units (Zergling, Hydralisk, Queen), but also that the opponent's units can counter some of yours (Hydralisk counters Marine, Marauder, Medivac; Queen counters Hellion, Reaper). Therefore, a balanced ground composition with supporting technology and economy is appropriate.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a stronger midgame army composition with upgrades and tech, while maintaining a robust economy and defensive structures. Be ready to adapt based on live scouting.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If you encounter a failure mode, recheck your resource allocation, ensure you are converting resources into army effectively, and adapt your composition based on live scouting. If supply blocked, build additional SupplyDepots. If the opponent's composition changes, re-evaluate your unit choices.

- Selection: Choose only a currently reachable candidate after checking live producers, prerequisites, resources, supply, and active queues; this is a candidate pool, not an ordered build list.
