- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

Human trajectories show a focus on ground units (Marine, Reaper, Hellion) and infrastructure (Barracks, Factory, Starport, SupplyDepot, SiegeTank). This supports a ground-macro strategy with heavy economy and technology.

- Verify that enemy air presence remains light or uncertain; if significant air is detected, adjust accordingly.
- Confirm that own production and technology are on track; if behind, prioritize catching up.
- Check that economy is sufficient to support increased production and technology; if not, consider expanding or optimizing workers.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army while increasing production and continuing technology development. Maintain economy and expansion posture, and stabilize tempo before further development.

Trajectory evidence indicates a heavy economy and technology posture for both sides. Ground forces are the primary domain, with possible pressure from Zergling-based compositions. Maintaining a strong ground army with supporting production and technology is consistent with the observed macro posture.
