## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase economy and technology, maintain expansion and defense, continue production, and maintain air presence.

The trajectory shows a heavy economy and technology investment with ground-oriented forces. The opponent's ground posture and heavy production suggest a need to strengthen ground capabilities while continuing to develop economy and tech to maintain an advantage.
