# N007 — ZvP Ground Macro Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase where both sides show a ground-oriented posture; own economy is heavy and expanding, while opponent has heavy production and technology with possible pressure. Strengthen ground army while increasing economy and maintaining expansion, production, and technology development. Maintain air presence and defense with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Stalker. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Own posture is ground-oriented with moderate production and light technology investment. Economy is heavy and expanding. Representative units include Zergling and Queen. Defense is light or uncertain, so live checks are needed.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

Human trajectory shows a standard Zerg macro opening: drones, overlords, extractor, queens, zerglings, creep tumors, roaches, spore crawlers, and Lair with zergling speed. This supports a ground-oriented macro style with defensive structures.

## Applicability Checks

- Check that own economy is indeed heavy and expanding before committing to further drones.
- Verify that opponent's ground posture is confirmed by scouting; if air presence is detected, adjust accordingly.
- Ensure that production facilities (Spawning Pool, Roach Warren) are available for the intended units.
- Confirm that Lair and zergling movement speed are researched or in progress to support the ground army.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army while increasing economy and maintaining expansion, production, and technology development. Maintain air presence and defense with safety checks.

Trajectory evidence shows a ground macro posture with heavy economy. Supported relations indicate Zergling and Roach can counter Stalker, but Stalker also counters Roach and Overlord. Therefore, a balanced ground composition with creep spread and defensive structures is prudent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition toward a stronger ground army with Lair tech and zergling movement speed, while maintaining economy and creep spread. Prepare for potential midgame pressure.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If resources are banked without army production, recheck production queues and prioritize spending. If repeated failed postures occur, reassess opponent composition and adjust tech choices.

## Knowledge-Grounded Execution Envelope

**Human-trajectory candidate pool (not an ordered build list):** Zergling, Queen, Overlord, Roach

- Selection: Choose only a currently reachable candidate after checking live producers, prerequisites, resources, supply, and active queues; this is a candidate pool, not an ordered build list.
- Resource conversion: When the combined bank is at least 750 and army supply is still below 15 after 05:00, prefer a currently executable combat candidate from this pool before optional greed or deeper technology.
- Unreachable-candidate fallback: If the preferred candidate is not reachable before the next decision, use a cheaper currently producible trajectory candidate; do not queue a long blocked prerequisite chain while army supply is low.
- Feedback repair: If the bank/low-army deficit persists for two decisions, stop repeating the same plan, re-read the best matching node, and choose a different reachable candidate or producer bottleneck repair.
