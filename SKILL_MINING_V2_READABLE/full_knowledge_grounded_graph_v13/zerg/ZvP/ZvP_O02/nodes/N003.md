# N003 — Maintain Economy/Expansion/Ground Posture with Safety Checks

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late midgame with light or uncertain economy, production, technology, and expansion posture for both sides; no reliable combat-unit cues observed. Maintain current economy, expansion, production, technology, army, defense, and air directions while developing with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include no reliably grounded combat-unit cue. Production appears light_or_uncertain, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your posture is broadly unknown-oriented with light or uncertain production and technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

Historical association indicates a default node with high support, suggesting that maintaining posture is a common and statistically sound choice in this context.

## Applicability Checks

- Verify that your economy is stable and you can support continued production.
- Confirm that your expansion count is appropriate for the current game phase.
- Ensure that your production facilities are active and not supply-blocked.
- Check that your technology upgrades are progressing without excessive resource sink.
- Assess enemy army composition and tech level using available intelligence.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current economy, expansion, production, technology, army, defense, and air directions while developing with safety checks.

Human trajectory evidence supports maintaining a stable posture without aggressive shifts, given the lack of reliable enemy information and the need to preserve flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing economy and expansion while keeping options open for ground or air transitions based on future enemy intelligence.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If any failure mode is detected, re-evaluate the current posture and adjust production or expansion accordingly. For resource-to-army conversion failure, focus on converting banked resources into army. For supply block, build a currently reachable knowledge-verified option or manage supply. For feedback not changing, consider a strategic shift. For composition mismatch, scout and adapt. For runtime instability, ensure API stability.
