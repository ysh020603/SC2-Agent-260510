# N007 — ZvP Early-Midgame Ground Macro vs Air Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with own ground-oriented macro posture and opponent air-oriented heavy economy/production posture. Continue economy and expansion development, strengthen ground army, increase technology, and maintain defense with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an air posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Phoenix, Oracle. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Own posture is ground-leaning with heavy economy and expansions, moderate production and technology. Representative units include Zergling and Queen. Air presence is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

Trajectory cues (CreepTumor, Drone, Overlord, Roach, EvolutionChamber, Extractor, Zergling, Queen, SporeCrawler, Hydralisk) indicate a ground-focused macro build with early anti-air investment (SporeCrawler) and tech progression toward Hydralisk.

## Applicability Checks

- Check if opponent has committed to air units (Phoenix/Oracle) via scouting.
- Check own economy and expansion status to ensure macro can continue.
- Check if anti-air units (SporeCrawler, Queen, Hydralisk) are available or in production.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue economy and expansion development, strengthen ground army, increase technology, and maintain defense with safety checks.

Trajectory evidence shows a ground macro posture with heavy economy. Opponent air posture suggests need for anti-air and scouting denial awareness. Knowledge supports Queen as counter to Oracle and Phoenix as threat to Overlords.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Develop a robust ground army with anti-air support (e.g., Hydralisk, SporeCrawler) while maintaining economy and tech progression.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If resources accumulate without army production, recheck production structures and larva usage. If repeated failed posture persists, reassess strategic direction.
