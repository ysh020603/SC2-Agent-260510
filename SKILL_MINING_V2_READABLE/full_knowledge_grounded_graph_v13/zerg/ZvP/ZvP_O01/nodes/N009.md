# N009 — Late Midgame Ground Macro vs Heavy Air Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In late midgame, own ground macro posture with heavy economy and production faces opponent heavy air posture with heavy economy, production, and technology. Strengthen ground army while maintaining defense and increasing economy and technology. Continue expansions and production. Maintain air direction.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an air posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot, Stalker, Phoenix, Oracle. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and technology investment. Representative units include Zergling, Queen, Overseer. Economy and expansion are heavy. Air presence is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

Trajectory cues (CreepTumor, Drone, Overlord, Roach, EvolutionChamber, Extractor, Zergling, Queen, SporeCrawler, Hydralisk) indicate a ground-oriented macro posture with heavy economy and production, consistent with the node's policy signature.

## Applicability Checks

- Check own economy and production status to ensure ability to continue macro.
- Check opponent air presence and composition to confirm heavy air posture.
- Check own anti-air capabilities (e.g., Queen, SporeCrawler) to ensure defense against air threats.
- Check technology progression to support required units and upgrades.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army while maintaining defense and increasing economy and technology. Continue expansions and production. Maintain air direction.

Trajectory evidence indicates a ground macro posture with heavy economy and production. Opponent air posture suggests need for anti-air capabilities, but own ground strength should be maintained. Knowledge supports ground units like Hydralisk and Roach countering ground units, while Queen and SporeCrawler provide anti-air. Maintain flexibility for adaptation.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing ground army with anti-air support, increase economy and technology, and maintain defensive structures.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If resource-to-army conversion failure is detected, recheck economy and production balance. If feedback not changing repeated failed posture, re-evaluate strategic direction based on live observations.
