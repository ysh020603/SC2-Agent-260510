# N011 — Midgame Ground-Macro Posture with Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

This node represents a midgame state where both sides have heavy economies and production, with the opponent showing a ground-leaning army and the own forces transitioning toward air. It is a default node, not a preferred or harmful label. Increase air presence and strengthen air forces while continuing economy and expansion development. Maintain production and continue technology and upgrades. Tempo is develop with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot, Stalker, Sentry. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown. The opponent's expansion posture is heavy, and pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and moderate technology investment. You have a heavy economy and defense, with moderate air presence. Representative units include Zergling, Mutalisk, and Queen. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

The trajectory action cues (Mutalisk, Drone, Overlord, CreepTumor, Zergling, Extractor, Hatchery, Corruptor, Queen, ZergFlyerWeaponsLevel1) indicate a balanced development of economy, production, and air tech. This supports the policy direction of increasing air while maintaining a strong economy.

## Applicability Checks

- Check if you have a Spire and Lair to produce Mutalisks and Corruptors.
- Check if you have sufficient gas income to support air unit production and upgrades.
- Check if your economy is strong enough to sustain both ground and air production.
- Check if the opponent has a significant number of Stalkers or other anti-air units that could threaten your air forces.
- Check if your overlord positioning is safe from Stalker snipes.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and strengthen air forces while continuing economy and expansion development. Maintain production and continue technology and upgrades. Tempo is develop with safety checks.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition toward a stronger air composition while maintaining a solid ground backbone and economy. Continue expanding and upgrading to support the air transition.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If you are failing to convert resources into army strength, recheck your production structures, larva usage, and supply. Also recheck if you are over-investing in economy at the expense of army. If the opponent's composition is countering your air, consider adjusting your air composition or adding ground support.
