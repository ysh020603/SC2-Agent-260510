# N010 — ZvP Midgame Ground-Macro with Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy and defense on both sides; opponent shows ground-heavy composition with possible pressure; own forces are ground-oriented with air tech emerging. Strengthen air presence while continuing economy and defense development; maintain production and technology progression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot, Stalker, Sentry, Observer. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and moderate technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

Human trajectory shows a mix of economy (Drone, Hatchery, Extractor), defense (Queen, CreepTumor), and air transition (Mutalisk, Corruptor, ZergFlyerWeaponsLevel1). This indicates a balanced approach with a focus on air superiority.

## Applicability Checks

- Check if you have a Spire and Lair to support Mutalisk/Corruptor production.
- Check if you have sufficient gas income for air units and upgrades.
- Check if your ground defense (Zerglings, Queens) is sufficient to hold against potential ground pushes.
- Check if you have adequate overlord spread and scouting to detect tech switches.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen air presence while continuing economy and defense development; maintain production and technology progression.

Trajectory evidence indicates a shift toward air units (Mutalisk, Corruptor) and flyer upgrades, while maintaining a heavy economy and defense. This supports a flexible transition to air dominance without neglecting ground defense.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Develop a robust air force (Mutalisk/Corruptor) with flyer upgrades, while maintaining a strong economy and defensive posture.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If resources are not being converted into army strength, recheck production and spending. If repeated failed postures occur, adjust strategy based on live observations.
