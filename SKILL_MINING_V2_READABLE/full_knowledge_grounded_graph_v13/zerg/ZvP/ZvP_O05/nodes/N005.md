# N005 — ZvP Late-Midgame Ground Macro Posture with Heavy Economy and Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Entered when both sides exhibit a heavy ground-oriented macro posture in the late midgame, with heavy economy, production, and technology investment, and a possible but not confirmed pressure posture. Maintain and strengthen the ground-oriented macro posture while continuing economy and expansion development, with safety checks against possible pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot, Stalker. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

The trajectory shows a heavy economy and defense posture with ground army focus, consistent with a macro-oriented strategy. The presence of units like Zergling, Roach, Hydralisk, Infestor, and SwarmHostMP suggests a flexible ground composition. The knowledge base indicates that Hydralisk and Roach have advantages against Zealot and Stalker, but also that Stalker can counter Roach and Zealot can counter Zergling, so composition choices should be informed by live scouting.

## Applicability Checks

- Check that your economy is indeed heavy and that you can sustain continued production and technology investment.
- Check that your defense posture is sufficient to handle possible pressure from a ground-heavy Protoss army.
- Check that you have adequate detection (e.g., Overseer) and anti-air capabilities given the moderate air presence possibility.
- Check that your current army composition is not overly vulnerable to the observed enemy units (e.g., avoid mass Zergling if Zealots are numerous).

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain and strengthen the ground-oriented macro posture while continuing economy and expansion development, with safety checks against possible pressure.

The trajectory evidence indicates a heavy economy and defense posture with ground army focus. The knowledge base supports that certain Zerg ground units (e.g., Hydralisk, Roach) have tactical advantages against the observed Protoss ground units (Zealot, Stalker), but also that Protoss units can counter some Zerg units (e.g., Stalker counters Roach, Zealot counters Zergling). Therefore, maintaining a flexible ground composition with appropriate counters is reasonable, while continuing to develop economy and technology.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition toward a more defined army composition and tech path based on live observation of enemy composition and pressure, while maintaining a strong economy and defense.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If you are failing to convert a strong economy into a sufficient army, re-evaluate your production structures, larva usage, and tech investments. Ensure that you are not banking excessive resources and that your army composition is appropriate for the observed enemy composition.
