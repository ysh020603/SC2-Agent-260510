# N002 — Economy / Expansion / Defense with Ground Macro

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game with moderate economy, heavy defense, and expansion posture; opponent posture unknown but technology heavy. Continue developing economy and defense while strengthening ground army; increase production and expansion; maintain air presence.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence consistent with unknown posture; no reliable combat-unit cues; production moderate, technology heavy, exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented macro posture with moderate production and light technology investment; representative units include Queen; expansion heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

Human trajectories show a mix of economy (Drone, Extractor), defense (Queen, CreepTumor), and ground tech (RoachWarren, HydraliskDen, BanelingNest) with Zergling and Roach production.

## Applicability Checks

- Verify that economy is on track with sufficient Drones and Hatcheries.
- Check that defensive structures (e.g., SpawningPool, RoachWarren) are in place.
- Ensure Overlord scouting is active to detect opponent tech or pressure.
- Confirm that Queen count is adequate for creep spread and defense.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing economy and defense while strengthening ground army; increase production and expansion; maintain air presence.

Trajectory evidence indicates a balanced development approach with emphasis on economy and defense, preserving flexibility for adaptation.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger ground army composition while maintaining economic lead and defensive readiness.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If resources are banked without army production, rebalance towards army. If repeated failed postures, reassess opponent information and adapt.

## Knowledge-Grounded Execution Envelope

**Human-trajectory candidate pool (not an ordered build list):** Queen, Overlord, Zergling, Roach

- Selection: Choose only a currently reachable candidate after checking live producers, prerequisites, resources, supply, and active queues; this is a candidate pool, not an ordered build list.
- Resource conversion: When the combined bank is at least 750 and army supply is still below 15 after 05:00, prefer a currently executable combat candidate from this pool before optional greed or deeper technology.
- Unreachable-candidate fallback: If the preferred candidate is not reachable before the next decision, use a cheaper currently producible trajectory candidate; do not queue a long blocked prerequisite chain while army supply is low.
- Feedback repair: If the bank/low-army deficit persists for two decisions, stop repeating the same plan, re-read the best matching node, and choose a different reachable candidate or producer bottleneck repair.
