# N011 — Late Midgame Ground Macro with Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In late midgame, both sides have heavy economy, production, and technology. Enemy ground posture with possible air presence; own ground macro with heavy air investment. Strengthen air forces while continuing economy and defense; maintain production and technology.

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot, Stalker, VoidRay. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

The trajectory shows production of Mutalisk, Corruptor, Queen, Zergling, and ZergFlyerWeaponsLevel1, indicating a transition to air with ground support. This aligns with the policy signature of strengthening air.

## Applicability Checks

- Verify that Spire and Lair are completed or in progress to support Mutalisk/Corruptor production.
- Check that economy can support heavy gas costs for air units and upgrades.
- Ensure sufficient ground forces (Zergling, Queen) to defend against Zealot/Stalker pressure.
- Confirm that Overlord supply is adequate for the army size.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen air forces while continuing economy and defense; maintain production and technology.

Trajectory evidence shows a shift toward air units (Mutalisk, Corruptor) and air upgrades (ZergFlyerWeaponsLevel1). Supported relations indicate Mutalisk and Queen counter VoidRay, while Stalker counters Mutalisk and Zealot counters Zergling/Queen. Thus, air investment is a reasonable response to enemy ground/air mix, but must be balanced with ground defense.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck
