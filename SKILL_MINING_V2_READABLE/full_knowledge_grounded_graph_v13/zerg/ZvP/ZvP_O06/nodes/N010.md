## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot, Stalker, Carrier. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown. The opponent has a heavy defense posture, heavy economy, and heavy expansion posture, with moderate air presence and a possible pressure posture.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. You have a heavy economy and expansion posture, and your army is ground-based with representative units including Zergling, Queen, and Overseer. Your air presence is light or uncertain, and you are in the late midgame phase with a possible pressure posture.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

The trajectory action cues (CreepTumor, Drone, Overlord, Roach, EvolutionChamber, Extractor, Zergling, Queen, SporeCrawler, Hydralisk) indicate a focus on economy, production, and ground army development, with some defensive structures (SporeCrawler) and tech (EvolutionChamber). This aligns with the node's heavy economy and ground posture.

## Applicability Checks

- Check that you have a sufficient economy to support continued expansion and production.
- Verify that your ground army composition is not being hard-countered by the opponent's observed units.
- Ensure that you have some anti-air capability (e.g., SporeCrawlers, Hydralisks) given the opponent's moderate air presence.
- Confirm that your technology development is on track to support your desired army composition.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain and strengthen your ground army, continue expanding and increasing your economy, continue production and technology development, and maintain defense. Develop with safety checks, and be prepared to adapt based on live observation.

The trajectory evidence indicates a heavy economy and expansion posture with ground-oriented army development. The knowledge base supports that your ground units (Hydralisk, Roach, Zergling) have counter relations against the opponent's representative units (Carrier, Stalker, Zealot), but also that the opponent's units (Stalker, Zealot) counter some of your units (Overlord, Overseer, Roach, Hydralisk, Queen, Zergling). Therefore, maintaining a strong ground army while continuing to expand and tech is consistent with the observed trajectory and the need to respond to potential threats.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a more solidified late-game composition, potentially incorporating more anti-air and upgrades, while maintaining a strong economy and production base.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If you experience a resource-to-army conversion failure, recheck your production and spending. If your composition is repeatedly countered, re-evaluate your unit mix and tech choices. If you encounter runtime or API instability, ensure your actions are robust to delays. If you have a live supply block with bank, prioritize spending on supply and production.
