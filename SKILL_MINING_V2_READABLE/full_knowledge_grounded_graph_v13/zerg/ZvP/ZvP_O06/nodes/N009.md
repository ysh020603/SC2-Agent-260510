# N009 — Late Midgame Ground Macro Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Both sides have developed heavy economies and ground-oriented armies with heavy production and technology. The opponent shows a ground posture with possible pressure, while your own posture is similarly ground-oriented with heavy defense. Maintain and strengthen the ground army while continuing economic and technological development, with safety checks against possible pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot, Stalker, Observer, Immortal. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

The trajectory shows a heavy economy and ground posture with units like Zergling, Hydralisk, Queen, Overseer, and SwarmHostMP. This suggests a defensive macro style with potential for harassment or late-game transitions.

## Applicability Checks

- Check that the opponent has not transitioned to a heavy air composition.
- Check that your economy is stable and you are not supply blocked.
- Check that your army composition is not severely countered by the opponent's visible units.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain and strengthen the ground army while continuing economic and technological development, with safety checks against possible pressure.

Human trajectory evidence indicates a heavy economy and ground posture on both sides. The knowledge base supports that Hydralisk and Roach have advantages against several Protoss ground units, but the opponent's Immortal and Stalker can threaten Roaches and air support. Therefore, maintain a balanced ground composition and keep detection and creep spread.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing the economy and technology while strengthening the ground army, possibly transitioning to a more advanced tech if the opponent shows a specific threat.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If you are floating resources without army production, or if repeated attempts to push fail, re-evaluate your composition and tech choices. Also recheck if the opponent has a composition that counters your current army.
