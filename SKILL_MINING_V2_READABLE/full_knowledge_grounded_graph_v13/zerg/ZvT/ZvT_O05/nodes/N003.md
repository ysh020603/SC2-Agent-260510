The trajectory actions (CreepTumor, Drone, Overlord, Zergling, EvolutionChamber, Extractor, Queen, Roach, Hatchery, Lair) indicate a focus on economy, creep spread, and tech progression. The presence of Roach and Lair suggests a transition towards a midgame ground army. The EvolutionChamber indicates early upgrades. This aligns with a heavy economy and technology posture.

- Check if you have at least 2 bases saturated with drones.
- Check if you have a Spawning Pool and at least one Queen for defense.

- Check if you have a Lair or are close to completing one.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue expanding and increasing economy while strengthening ground army and increasing technology. Maintain defense and air presence. Develop with safety checks.

The trajectory evidence indicates a heavy economy and expansion posture, with moderate production and heavy technology. The opponent's ground-heavy posture with possible pressure suggests that a balanced approach of economy, production, and technology is appropriate. The knowledge base supports that Zergling and Queen are effective against early Terran ground units, but caution is needed against Hellion and Reaper harassment.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a stronger ground army composition, potentially incorporating Roach, while continuing to expand and tech up. Maintain creep spread and overlord positioning to secure map vision.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If you are supply blocked with a large bank, immediately spend resources on army and production structures. If you repeatedly lose to early pressure, re-evaluate your drone count and consider building more army earlier. If the opponent shows a heavy air composition, transition to anti-air units and static defense.
