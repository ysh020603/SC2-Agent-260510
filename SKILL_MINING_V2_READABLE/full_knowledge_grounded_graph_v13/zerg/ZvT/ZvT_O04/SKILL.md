## Opening Strategy

This opening focuses on establishing a strong economic foundation, teching up, and expanding, while maintaining a moderate production capacity. The army composition is ground-leaning, and the strategy remains flexible to adapt to live observations.

Develop an economy / upgrade / expansion posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: light_or_uncertain
- Army direction: ground-leaning
