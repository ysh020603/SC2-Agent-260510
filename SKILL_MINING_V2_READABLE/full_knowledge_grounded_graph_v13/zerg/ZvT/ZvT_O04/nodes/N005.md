# N005 — Economy/Upgrade/Expansion Posture with Ground Macro

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late midgame with heavy economy and production on both sides; opponent shows ground macro posture with SiegeTank, Marine, Medivac cues. Continue economy and expansion development while strengthening ground army and maintaining defense; increase technology and upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Terran ground macro posture with heavy production and technology; representative cues include SiegeTank, Marine, Medivac; air presence moderate but ground-leaning.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Zerg ground macro posture with heavy economy, production, and technology; representative units include Zergling, Mutalisk, Queen; creep spread and upgrades in progress.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

Human trajectories show a heavy economy/upgrade/expansion posture with ground army focus. The presence of Zergling, Mutalisk, Queen, and structures like EvolutionChamber and Lair indicates a balanced tech and production approach.

## Applicability Checks

- Check if economy is still heavy and expansions are safe.
- Check if opponent's ground army is still the primary threat.
- Check if upgrades and tech are progressing as planned.
- Check if creep spread is adequate for defense and map control.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue economy and expansion development while strengthening ground army and maintaining defense; increase technology and upgrades.

Trajectory evidence indicates a heavy economy/upgrade/expansion opening. The opponent's ground posture suggests focusing on ground strength and tech to maintain advantage, while preserving flexibility for air transitions if needed.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain economy lead while transitioning to a stronger ground composition with upgrades; be ready to adapt to opponent's tech or air switches.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If resources are not converting into army strength, recheck production and tech priorities; if repeated failed posture, adjust strategy based on live observations.
