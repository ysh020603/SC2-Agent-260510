# N004 — Maintain Economy/Ground/Expansion Posture with Safety Checks

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late midgame with unknown opponent posture and light/uncertain production and tech; maintain current path while checking for threats. Maintain current economy/ground/expansion path while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include no reliably grounded combat-unit cue. Production appears light_or_uncertain, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Own posture is broadly unknown-oriented with light/uncertain production and tech; economy and expansion are light/uncertain. Check live state before acting.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

Historical association indicates a default node with no strong positive or negative bias; maintain current path.

## Applicability Checks

- Check current supply and resource bank to avoid supply blocks.
- Verify that production structures are active and not idle.
- Confirm that economy and expansion are on track with the intended posture.
- Monitor enemy intel for any signs of a shift in composition or pressure.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current economy/ground/expansion path while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

Trajectory evidence supports maintaining the current posture; no strong signal to deviate. Preserve flexibility to adapt based on live observations.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing economy/ground/expansion while keeping options open for tech or army shifts based on enemy intel.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

Recheck if supply block occurs with banked resources, or if repeated attempts to push fail; adjust production and tech accordingly.
