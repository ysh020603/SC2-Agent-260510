## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen air while continuing economy, defense, expansion, and technology development; maintain production with safety checks.

Trajectory evidence shows a shift toward air (Mutalisk, ZergFlyerWeaponsLevel1) while maintaining a heavy ground/economy base. Knowledge supports Mutalisk synergy with ground units and upgrades, and counters against Queen, but Queen also counters Mutalisk, so timing and numbers matter.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
