# N001 — Economy / Expansion / Ground Opening

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

Early game with heavy economy and expansion posture, ground-oriented army, moderate production, light or uncertain technology. Enemy intelligence shows a Queen, suggesting a ground macro posture. Strengthen ground army, increase economy, maintain expansion, maintain production, continue technology, maintain defense, maintain air direction, develop with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with moderate production and light_or_uncertain technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

Human trajectories in this node frequently include Drones, Overlords, Roaches, Zerglings, Queens, and upgrades like zerglingmovementspeed and GlialReconstitution, indicating a balanced ground macro approach.

## Applicability Checks

- Check if you have at least one Hatchery and sufficient larvae for Drones.
- Check if Spawning Pool is built or in progress to enable Queens and Zerglings.
- Check if you have sufficient resources for desired expansions and units.
- Check if enemy composition is still ground-oriented (no air or heavy tech).

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase economy, maintain expansion, maintain production, continue technology, maintain defense, maintain air direction, develop with safety checks.

Trajectory evidence shows a heavy economy and expansion focus with ground units and upgrades. This supports a macro-oriented ground game, leveraging Queens for defense and creep, and Roaches/Zerglings for army composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger ground army with upgrades (e.g., Glial Reconstitution, Zerg Missile Weapons) while maintaining economy and expansion lead.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If resources are banked without army production, re-evaluate unit composition and production structures. If repeated failed posture, adjust strategy based on live scouting.
