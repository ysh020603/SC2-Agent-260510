# V2 Enemy Response

Enemy Intelligence is remembered observation, not omniscient live vision. Weight evidence by relevance:

- a current repeated composition or enabling tech structure is strong evidence;
- a threat flag is a warning that should be combined with observed assets;
- one old isolated unit is weak evidence and normally justifies only a hedge.

For `A counters B`, A is the candidate counter and B is the threatened target. Filter counter evidence by our race, allowed outputs, current or reachable producers, cost, supply, and arrival time. Prefer a candidate that can reinforce the current strategy over a distant full technology pivot unless the current army cannot interact with the threat.

First check the attack layer. Structured `Unit.weapons.target_type` is authoritative:

- a direct anti-air response must have an Air or Any weapon;
- a direct anti-ground response must have a Ground or Any weapon;
- a detector or spell-support unit may solve detection or control, but cannot fill the direct-damage response score unless it also has the required weapon;
- discard broad ontology-expanded counter relations that claim a ground-only unit counters a flying target.

When the Derived Planning Snapshot reports a layer gap, meet its response score with verified trainable units or armed static defense while preserving a feasible producer/prerequisite route.
