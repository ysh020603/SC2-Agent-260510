# Summary

Open with a Reaper into a fast natural, then establish Barracks, Factory, and Starport production around Marines, Marauders, WidowMines, Medivacs, and a small SiegeTank core. Prioritize Stimpack, CombatShield, infantry upgrades, reactor-heavy production, and steady expansions. Attack after the bio count and first mines are established, then scale production and economy instead of committing to a one-base all-in.
