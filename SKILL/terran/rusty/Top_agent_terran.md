# Summary

Old Rusty opens with a standard depot-into-rax gateway, grabbing an early orbital and natural CC to set up a two-base economy before laying down a factory with tech lab for tank production and a starport to unlock air support. The build scales into a 5-rax production core with mixed tech labs and reactors, pairing combat shield research with a steady tank-medivac-viking backbone while keeping marine flood as the mineral dump; the playstyle leans into a sustained macro push with siege mode anchoring the ground and medivacs enabling drop pressure across the map. By the time the economy stretches to 3+ bases and the starport runs medivac-viking cycles alongside factory tank output, the army aims for a maxed-out bio-mech deathball that overwhelms through raw production volume and siege control rather than any sharp timing or late-game tech switch.
