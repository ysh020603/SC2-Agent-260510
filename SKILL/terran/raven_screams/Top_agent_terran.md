# Summary

The opening rolls out with a standard rax-first into expand, grabbing gas early to power a Factory and Starport follow-up while dropping a bunker for safety and morphing both CCs into orbitals to juice the economy. Production scales hard with 3 rax, a Factory tech-labbed for tanks, and a Starport bouncing between tech lab and reactor to churn banshees, libs, and vikings; key upgrades include banshee cloak and speed, combat shield, and air +1, creating a harass-heavy mid-game that leans on mobile air units and siege tanks to control space while bio floods out. The late-game aims for a maxed-out 3-base air-mech-bio hybrid with sustained production from a Raven-boosted Starport, an Armory unlocking higher-tier options, and a gas-hungry army of banshees, liberators, vikings, tanks, and marines that grinds opponents down through relentless multi-prong pressure and upgrade advantage.
