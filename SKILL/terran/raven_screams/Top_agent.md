# Summary

**Against Terran:** The opening rolls out with a standard rax-first into expand, grabbing gas early to power a Factory and Starport follow-up while dropping a bunker for safety and morphing both CCs into orbitals to juice the economy. Production scales hard with 3 rax, a Factory tech-labbed for tanks, and a Starport bouncing between tech lab and reactor to churn banshees, libs, and vikings; key upgrades include banshee cloak and speed, combat shield, and air +1, creating a harass-heavy mid-game that leans on mobile air units and siege tanks to control space while bio floods out. The late-game aims for a maxed-out 3-base air-mech-bio hybrid with sustained production from a Raven-boosted Starport, an Armory unlocking higher-tier options, and a gas-hungry army of banshees, liberators, vikings, tanks, and marines that grinds opponents down through relentless multi-prong pressure and upgrade advantage.

**Against Protoss:** This build opens with a standard rax-first into a fast natural CC, grabbing early gas and quickly cycling into a Factory with a tech lab for tank production while dropping a Starport with its own tech lab to kick off the Banshee cloak transition. The mid-game scales into a 3-base setup with a Reactor Starport and multiple rax, pumping a mixed bio-heavy force supported by cloaked Banshees, Liberators, and Ravens with Corvid Reactor while layering combat shield, ship weapons, and an Armory for upgrade versatility. The late-game aims to max out on a sky-terran-lite composition with sustained Banshee, Viking, and Liberator production alongside a marine-tank backbone, leveraging Raven utility and multi-pronged air harassment to grind down opponents through overwhelming map pressure and production volume.

**Against Zerg:** The build opens with a rax-first into quick natural CC, grabbing early gas and rolling into a Factory and Starport with a tech lab on each to unlock tank and banshee tech while shoringing up with a bunker. Production scales through a 3-rax core with mixed tech labs and reactors, powering a bio-mech-air hybrid that leans on cloak banshees for harass while layering in tanks, libs, and eventually marauders as upgrades like combat shield, banshee speed, and raven corvid reactor come online. The endgame aims for a 4-base economy flooding a maxed-out mixed army of marine-marauder-tank supported by mass banshee-lib-viking air and raven utility, overwhelming with sustained production and upgrade-backed multi-prong pressure rather than a single all-in timing.
