# Summary

This build opens with a standard rax-first into a fast natural CC, grabbing early gas and quickly cycling into a Factory with a tech lab for tank production while dropping a Starport with its own tech lab to kick off the Banshee cloak transition. The mid-game scales into a 3-base setup with a Reactor Starport and multiple rax, pumping a mixed bio-heavy force supported by cloaked Banshees, Liberators, and Ravens with Corvid Reactor while layering combat shield, ship weapons, and an Armory for upgrade versatility. The late-game aims to max out on a sky-terran-lite composition with sustained Banshee, Viking, and Liberator production alongside a marine-tank backbone, leveraging Raven utility and multi-pronged air harassment to grind down opponents through overwhelming map pressure and production volume.
