# Summary

The build opens with a standard rax-first into expand, grabbing an early reaper for scout and map presence before pivoting into a Factory with a tech lab to start siege tank production while dropping the natural CC to kickstart the economy. From there it scales into a heavy mech setup with multiple factories running tech labs, an Armory for vehicle upgrades with mech +1, and a Starport with a reactor as the gas economy deepens, pumping out tanks and thors while maintaining OC energy and depot discipline to avoid supply blocks. The endgame floods production with a massive factory and starport backbone across a wide mineral base network, aiming for a gas-heavy maxed mech composition of thors, siege tanks, and a full viking squadron that leverages overwhelming sustained production and vehicle upgrades to siege out the opponent.
