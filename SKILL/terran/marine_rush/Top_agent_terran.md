# Summary

This build goes rax-first with no gas mining, skipping the natural CC entirely to slam down 3 rax on a single base and start pumping Marines immediately while staying ahead of supply with constant depots. There is no mid-game scaling beyond adding more rax and depots—no tech lab, no stim, no combat shields, no factory or starport—just pure mineral dump into Marine production with the Orbital energy used only for worker saturation. The endgame plan is to hit a critical mass of Marines and end the game before any splash damage or tech units come online, relying on overwhelming production tempo from 4+ rax to flood the opponent in a single all-in wave.

# Details

[Step 1] Keep SCV production rolling to saturate the mineral lines while throwing down depots to stay ahead of supply. Drop 3 rax for a bio production backbone, pacing the builds so minerals flow cleanly between workers, supply, and the new production without stalling out.

[Step 2] Morph the CC into an orbital to get the energy economy rolling, then add 2 rax alongside the existing one to open up bio production. Train a wave of 6 Marines while keeping SCV production steady to push worker saturation forward. Make sure to pace depot construction ahead of the supply curve since this step dumps a lot of mineral-heavy units and buildings into the queue.

[Step 3] Keep SCV production rolling while throwing down a depot to stay ahead of supply, then pump out a wave of Marines to build a solid bio foundation. Drop another rax for extra production capacity and cap it off with another depot so the growing army and worker count don't get supply blocked.

[Step 4] Train a wave of 9 Marines across the rax to keep production tempo-ready production flowing, and throw down a depot to stay ahead of the supply curve as the barracks cycle continues.

[Step 5] Throw down a depot to keep supply headroom healthy for the marine flood, then pour minerals into a wave of marines from the rax. Keep the production cycle tight so the army swells quickly without getting supply blocked.

[Step 6] Queue up a wave of Marines across all active rax to keep the barracks humming and build out a solid bio core. Make sure you've got depot space coming online to handle this production spike without getting supply blocked.

[Step 7] Flood out a wave of Marines to build a critical mass while throwing down 3 depots to keep supply from capping. The rax should be churning constantly now, so keep the mineral pipeline flowing and make sure there's enough supply headroom for the next production cycle without overbuilding unused cap.

[Step 8] This is a classic one-base Marine all-in cheese that commits everything to a single-game-ending timing, riding pure production tempo and mineral dump to drown the opponent before any tech answers come online. Use this gameplan as your strategic baseline -- adapt your decisions based on what you scout and how the game unfolds.
