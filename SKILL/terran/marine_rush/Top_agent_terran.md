# Summary

The bot opens with a rax-first into fast OC on the main, skipping gas entirely to stay mineral-heavy and dumping everything into SCV production while layering depots to stay ahead of supply. The mid-game is pure bio macro with no tech labs, no upgrades, and no factory or starport—just a flood of rax adding up to mass marine production with reactors nowhere in sight, playing a relentless reinforcement-heavy style that banks on overwhelming numbers rather than tech timings. The late-game plan never pivots: it stays on the gasless marine flood, pushing toward a maxed-out bio ball with no splash support, no medivacs, and no transition, relying purely on raw unit count and constant reinforcements to drown the opponent under an endless tide of marines.
