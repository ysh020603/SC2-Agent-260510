# Summary

The opening goes depot into rax with two Refineries for gas, morphing the CC into an orbital before adding a factory and starport to establish a Raven-opening tech path while grabbing an expansion CC and pumping reapers and hellions for early map presence. Production scales through a 3-rax, 2-port, 2-factory setup with tech labs on the factory and starport, reactors on rax and one port, powering a bio-mech hybrid of marines, tanks, medivacs, and vikings while stim, combat shield, and +1/+1 bio upgrades come online alongside mech +1 vehicle weapons. The late-game aims to leverage a 4-base economy with an armory unlocking armor upgrades to support a maxed-out timing push built around a massive marine-tank-viking core with Raven support, overwhelming through sustained production and upgrade advantage rather than a hard tech switch.
