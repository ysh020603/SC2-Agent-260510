# Summary

**Against Terran:** The opening goes depot into rax with two Refineries for gas, morphing the CC into an orbital before adding a factory and starport to establish a Raven-opening tech path while grabbing an expansion CC and pumping reapers and hellions for early map presence. Production scales through a 3-rax, 2-port, 2-factory setup with tech labs on the factory and starport, reactors on rax and one port, powering a bio-mech hybrid of marines, tanks, medivacs, and vikings while stim, combat shield, and +1/+1 bio upgrades come online alongside mech +1 vehicle weapons. The late-game aims to leverage a 4-base economy with an armory unlocking armor upgrades to support a maxed-out timing push built around a massive marine-tank-viking core with Raven support, overwhelming through sustained production and upgrade advantage rather than a hard tech switch.

**Against Protoss:** The build opens with a standard rax-first into reaper-hellion map presence, grabbing an early natural CC and following up with a factory into starport for Raven and siege tank tech. Production scales hard with a 5-rax core backed by 2 starports and a tech-labbed factory, pumping a marine-tank-viking-medivac backbone while layering stim, combat shields, and +1/+1 bio upgrades alongside vehicle weapons for the mech splash. The economy balloons to support 4+ bases with 4 e-bays and an armory, aiming for a maxed-out marine-tank-viking deathball with liberator transition that can siege forward or defend all-ins with Raven support and overwhelming reinforcements.

**Against Zerg:** The build opens with a standard rax-first into factory, cranking out reapers and hellions for early map presence while dropping a CC to get the orbital and mule economy rolling, then adds a starport with a tech lab to start the Raven and tank production that anchors this defensive opening. Production scales hard with a 5-rax bio core backed by dual-starport medivac and viking output, pushing stim and combat shield while sieging up with tanks and liberators to control space and force favorable trades. The endgame aims for a maxed-out bio-mech deathball with +1/+1 infantry, vehicle weapons, and an armory unlocking mech upgrades, supported by 3+ bases of saturation and overwhelming sustained production to grind out the opponent through superior macro and multi-pronged pressure.
