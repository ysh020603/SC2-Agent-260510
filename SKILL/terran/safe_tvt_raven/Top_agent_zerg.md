# Summary

The build opens with a standard rax-first into factory, cranking out reapers and hellions for early map presence while dropping a CC to get the orbital and mule economy rolling, then adds a starport with a tech lab to start the Raven and tank production that anchors this defensive opening. Production scales hard with a 5-rax bio core backed by dual-starport medivac and viking output, pushing stim and combat shield while sieging up with tanks and liberators to control space and force favorable trades. The endgame aims for a maxed-out bio-mech deathball with +1/+1 infantry, vehicle weapons, and an armory unlocking mech upgrades, supported by 3+ bases of saturation and overwhelming sustained production to grind out the opponent through superior macro and multi-pronged pressure.
