# Summary

The build opens with a standard rax-first into quick orbital, getting a reaper out for early scout pressure while taking a fast natural to set up the macro backbone, then layers on a factory and starport behind stim and combat shield to open the bio-mine-medivac tech path. Production scales hard into a 5-rax setup with a mix of tech labs and reactors, pumping out a stim bio core supported by widow mines and medivacs, with concussive shells and +1 attack rolling to enable aggressive skirmishing and multi-prong drop play across the map. The economy balloons out to 3 bases with refineries to fuel the gas-hungry composition, aiming for a maxed-out bio-mine timing push with constant reinforcement that can either crack the opponent through sustained pressure or pivot into further upgrades and tech transitions if the game drags on.
