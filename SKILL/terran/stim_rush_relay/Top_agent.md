# Summary

**Against Terran:** The build opens with a standard rax-first into orbital timing, grabbing early gas for stim and quickly adding a reactor as the natural goes down, then pivoting into a factory and starport for the classic 1-1-1 shell with widow mine defense and medivac follow-up. Production scales aggressively through a 5-rax setup with multiple reactors and tech labs, powering a marine-marauder core supported by medivac drops and widow mine harassment, while stim, combat shield, concussive shells, and +1/+1 armor upgrades come online to keep the bio ball sharp. The endgame aims for a massive 3-base economy fueling pure marine-marauder flood production, with the starport cranking medivacs to keep the deathball healed and mobile, looking to overwhelm with a maxed-out timing or sustained pressure rather than any tech switch.

**Against Protoss:** The build opens with a standard rax-first into quick expansion, grabbing early gas for a tech lab and reaper scout before transitioning into a Factory and Starport to unlock the full 1-1-1 suite while keeping worker production rolling. Production scales up to 5 rax with a mix of tech labs and reactors alongside a reactor Starport, powering a stim-combat shield-concussive shell bio core supported by medivacs and widow mines while +1 attack and armor roll at the ebay. The endgame plan is to max out on a marine-marauder-medivac ball across 3 bases with OCs fueling mule-driven macro, aiming to overwhelm with a sustained production flood rather than a sharp timing.

**Against Zerg:** The build opens with a standard rax-first into quick orbital, getting a reaper out for early scout pressure while taking a fast natural to set up the macro backbone, then layers on a factory and starport behind stim and combat shield to open the bio-mine-medivac tech path. Production scales hard into a 5-rax setup with a mix of tech labs and reactors, pumping out a stim bio core supported by widow mines and medivacs, with concussive shells and +1 attack rolling to enable aggressive skirmishing and multi-prong drop play across the map. The economy balloons out to 3 bases with refineries to fuel the gas-hungry composition, aiming for a maxed-out bio-mine timing push with constant reinforcement that can either crack the opponent through sustained pressure or pivot into further upgrades and tech transitions if the game drags on.
