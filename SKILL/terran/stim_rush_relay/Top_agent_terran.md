# Summary

The build opens with a standard rax-first into orbital timing, grabbing early gas for stim and quickly adding a reactor as the natural goes down, then pivoting into a factory and starport for the classic 1-1-1 shell with widow mine defense and medivac follow-up. Production scales aggressively through a 5-rax setup with multiple reactors and tech labs, powering a marine-marauder core supported by medivac drops and widow mine harassment, while stim, combat shield, concussive shells, and +1/+1 armor upgrades come online to keep the bio ball sharp. The endgame aims for a massive 3-base economy fueling pure marine-marauder flood production, with the starport cranking medivacs to keep the deathball healed and mobile, looking to overwhelm with a maxed-out timing or sustained pressure rather than any tech switch.
