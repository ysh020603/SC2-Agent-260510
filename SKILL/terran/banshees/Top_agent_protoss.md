Production scales through a 4-rax setup with mixed tech labs and reactors while the Factory and dual Starport output tanks and banshees, banking on combat shield for the bio backbone and a steady stream of cloaked air harassment to keep the opponent honest across the map. The endgame aims for a maxed-out 3-base economy supporting a chunky bio-tank-banshee deathball, overwhelming with sustained mixed-tech production rather than a hard transition, keeping gas flexing between siege mode upgrades and continued banshee cloak pressure to close out the game.
