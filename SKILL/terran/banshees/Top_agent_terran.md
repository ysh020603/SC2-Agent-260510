Production scales through 3 rax with a split of tech labs and reactors, supported by 2 Starports and a Factory pumping tanks and banshees, with combat shields coming online to bulk up the marine backbone and the playstyle leaning into a harassment-heavy tempo with banshees while the bio-tank core controls space and forces reactions. The build aims for a gas-heavy mixed comp of marine-tank-banshee sustained across 3+ bases with orbitals fueling mule economy, eventually maxing out on a versatile army that can siege, drop, and pick off tech structures, with the banshee count scaling into a critical mass that demands splash or detection answers while the tank line entrenches and marines flood the front.
