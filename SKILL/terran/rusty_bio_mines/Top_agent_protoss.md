# Summary

The opening plays out as a standard rax-first into quick expansion, grabbing gas early to afford a tech lab reaper opening while stim and a reactor kick in on the main barracks, then layering a factory and starport behind it to set up the bio-mine core. Production scales through a 5-rax backbone with double e-bays running +1/+1, reactors cycling marines and widow mines while the starport mixes medivacs and vikings; the playstyle leans on map control with burrowed mines and drop harass, eventually adding ghost academy and armory to layer in EMP and higher-tier upgrades. The endgame aims for a maxed-out bio-mine-ghost deathball supported by 4+ orbitals for scan and mule energy, with the economy spreading across 4+ bases to feed constant reinforcement and overwhelming sustained production through the multi-rax infrastructure.
