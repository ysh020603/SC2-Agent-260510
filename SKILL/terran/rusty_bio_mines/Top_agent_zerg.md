# Summary

The build opens with a depot-first into 2 rax and a pair of refineries, getting an orbital up before taking the natural and splitting add-ons between a tech lab for stim and a reactor for marine production, then transitioning into factory-starport with a factory reactor to start pumping widow mines. Production scales through a 5 rax core with tech labs and reactors spread around, stim and combat shield finishing while concussive shells come online, and the army leans into a bio-mine drop style with medivacs for mobility and widow mines for map control and defensive coverage, taking a 3 base economy and adding an ebay for +1 upgrades alongside a ghost academy. The endgame pushes toward a maxed bio-mine-ghost composition with sustained medivac production from a reactor starport, multiple orbitals for scan and mule economy, and the ghost academy opening up EMP and nukes to break late-game engagements against heavy spellcaster or armored compositions.
