# Summary

The opening rolls out a depot-first into dual rax with early gas, getting an orbital up quick for macro tools and scouting with a reaper before adding a tech lab for stim and dropping a factory-reactor and starport to set up the bio-mine core. Production scales hard across 5+ rax with dual ebays kicking off +1/+1, combat shields and concussive shells for the bio ball, while the factory spits widow mines for map control and the starport mixes medivacs and vikings to support a mobile, harass-heavy playstyle with drop potential. The endgame pushes toward a maxed-out bio-mine force backed by ghosts for spellcaster utility, sustained by a 3-base economy with an armory unlocking higher-tier upgrades, aiming to overwhelm with constant marine-marauder production and defensive mine coverage rather than a hard tech switch.
