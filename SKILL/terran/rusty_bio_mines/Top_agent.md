# Summary

**Against Terran:** The opening rolls out a depot-first into dual rax with early gas, getting an orbital up quick for macro tools and scouting with a reaper before adding a tech lab for stim and dropping a factory-reactor and starport to set up the bio-mine core. Production scales hard across 5+ rax with dual ebays kicking off +1/+1, combat shields and concussive shells for the bio ball, while the factory spits widow mines for map control and the starport mixes medivacs and vikings to support a mobile, harass-heavy playstyle with drop potential. The endgame pushes toward a maxed-out bio-mine force backed by ghosts for spellcaster utility, sustained by a 3-base economy with an armory unlocking higher-tier upgrades, aiming to overwhelm with constant marine-marauder production and defensive mine coverage rather than a hard tech switch.

**Against Protoss:** The opening plays out as a standard rax-first into quick expansion, grabbing gas early to afford a tech lab reaper opening while stim and a reactor kick in on the main barracks, then layering a factory and starport behind it to set up the bio-mine core. Production scales through a 5-rax backbone with double e-bays running +1/+1, reactors cycling marines and widow mines while the starport mixes medivacs and vikings; the playstyle leans on map control with burrowed mines and drop harass, eventually adding ghost academy and armory to layer in EMP and higher-tier upgrades. The endgame aims for a maxed-out bio-mine-ghost deathball supported by 4+ orbitals for scan and mule energy, with the economy spreading across 4+ bases to feed constant reinforcement and overwhelming sustained production through the multi-rax infrastructure.

**Against Zerg:** The build opens with a depot-first into 2 rax and a pair of refineries, getting an orbital up before taking the natural and splitting add-ons between a tech lab for stim and a reactor for marine production, then transitioning into factory-starport with a factory reactor to start pumping widow mines. Production scales through a 5 rax core with tech labs and reactors spread around, stim and combat shield finishing while concussive shells come online, and the army leans into a bio-mine drop style with medivacs for mobility and widow mines for map control and defensive coverage, taking a 3 base economy and adding an ebay for +1 upgrades alongside a ghost academy. The endgame pushes toward a maxed bio-mine-ghost composition with sustained medivac production from a reactor starport, multiple orbitals for scan and mule economy, and the ghost academy opening up EMP and nukes to break late-game engagements against heavy spellcaster or armored compositions.
