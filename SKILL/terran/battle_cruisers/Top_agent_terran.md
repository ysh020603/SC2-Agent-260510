# Summary

This build opens with a standard rax-first into expand, grabbing gas early to power a Factory into Starport tech path that quickly pivots toward a Fusion Core and Battlecruiser transition while mixing in Siege Tanks for ground defense. Production scales up with a 5-rax core backed by a tech-labbed Factory and Starport, flooding Marines with a sprinkle of Tanks and a steady BC cadence as Combat Shield comes online, playing a macro-heavy defensive posture that leans on the BC to stabilize before the bio ball gets scary. The endgame aims for a 3-base economy supporting a chunky bio-mech-air hybrid, eventually fielding a maxed-out army with multiple BCs anchoring the sky while the Marine-Tank backbone floods the ground, looking to overwhelm with sustained production rather than a sharp timing.
