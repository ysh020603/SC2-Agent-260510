# Summary

Flying Rust opens with a rax-first into quick OC, dropping a fast natural CC on Kairos Junction to get the eco rolling while adding a Factory and Starport to set up the tech backbone, with early marine production and a defensive bunker to hold the fort. The mid-game scales into a multi-rax bio core backed by Factory tech lab tanks and a Fusion Core, kicking off combat shield and churning out BCs from a double tech lab Starport setup to build that gas-heavy air fleet while marines flood the map. By the late game the economy supports a 3-base saturation pumping a maxed-out MMM+Tank+BC deathball, leaning on sustained production across 5+ rax and 2 ports to overwhelm with endless marine reinforcement and a swelling battlecruiser armada that forces the opponent to deal with both sky and ground terrors simultaneously.
