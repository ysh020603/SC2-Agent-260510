# Summary

The opening is a standard rax-first into quick CC expansion with an early Refinery, flowing into Factory and Starport while keeping marine production rolling and dropping a bunker for hold defense. The build scales into 4+ rax with mixed tech labs and reactors, pumping stim-less bio reinforced by siege tanks and a Fusion Core unlock, while combat shield comes online and the air transition begins with cloaked banshee potential into BC production. The endgame caps out with a gas-heavy sky-terran deathball of mass BCs supported by tank and marine filler, sustaining production across 3+ bases with constant MULE energy from multiple Orbitals, aiming to overwhelm with unanswerable air dominance and Yamato-backed attrition.
