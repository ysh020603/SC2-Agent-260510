# Summary

**Against Terran:** The build opens with a standard Barracks-first into quick expand, getting gas online early and transitioning through a Factory before adding a Starport to set up the core tech triangle. Production scales into a two-base setup with FactoryTechLab pumping SiegeTanks, StarportTechLab producing Liberators, Medivacs, and Ravens, and BarracksTechLab enabling Stimpack and ShieldWall while Marines form the bio-mech screen. The endgame aims to max out on a SiegeTank-Marine-Liberator core with Raven support, leveraging a saturated two-base economy to hit a power spike with upgraded bio and enough air mechanics to lock down space and force favorable trades.

**Against Protoss:** The opening goes SupplyDepot into Barracks with an early Refinery, getting an OrbitalCommand on the home CommandCenter before taking an expansion and adding FactoryTechLab followed by Starport, setting up a two-base economy that churns out early Marines while starting the SiegeTank count. Production scales with two Barracks, one BarracksTechLab for Stimpack and ShieldWall, FactoryTechLab pumping SiegeTanks and WidowMines, and StarportTechLab producing Ravens, Liberators, and Medivacs after CorvidReactor. The endgame aims to max out on a Marine-SiegeTank core with Liberator and Raven support, pushing for a three-base economy that applies WidowMine drops and siege lines until the opponent cracks under mixed aggression.

**Against Zerg:** The build opens with a standard Barracks-first into quick CommandCenter expansion, taking two copies of Refinery and a Factory to set up a gas-heavy tech transition while keeping Marine production rolling for early defense. By mid-game the player scales into two Barracks, two Factories, and a Starport with the specific BarracksTechLab, FactoryTechLab, and StarportTechLab add-ons required by its units and upgrades. Produce Marines, SiegeTanks, WidowMines, Liberators, and Ravens while researching Stimpack, ShieldWall, and CorvidReactor to enable area denial. The late game aims for a maxed two-base timing with sustained SiegeTank, Liberator, and Raven production, using the bio backbone to screen while the mech core controls space.
