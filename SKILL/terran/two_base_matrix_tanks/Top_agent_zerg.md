# Summary

The build opens with a standard rax-first into quick CC-first expansion, dropping 2 refineries and a factory to set up a gas-heavy tech transition while keeping marine production rolling for early defense. By mid-game the player scales into 2 rax, 2 factories, and a starport loaded with tech labs, pushing out a bio-mech hybrid of marines, tanks, widow mines, liberators, and ravens while grabbing stim, combat shield, and Corvid Reactor to enable a harass-heavy, area-denial playstyle. The late-game aims for a maxed-out 2-base timing with overwhelming sustained production of siege tanks, liberators, and raven support, using the bio backbone to screen while the mech core controls space and the raven provides matrix and anti-caster utility.

# Details

[Step 1] Throw down a depot and keep SCV production rolling to saturate the economy, then add a rax and a Refinery to build out gas flexibility for upcoming tech. Drop another depot to keep supply headroom healthy as worker and production demand scales up.

[Step 2] Morph the main CC into an orbital and immediately drop another CC to establish a two-base economy, keeping SCV production rolling to saturate the expanding infrastructure. Add a Refinery to strengthen gas flexibility while throwing down a Factory for the upcoming tech transition, and round out the phase with a few Marines to maintain a modest defense presence.

[Step 3] Keep several SCVs coming while throwing down a couple of depots to stay ahead of the supply curve as production ramps up. Add a factory with a tech lab and morph the main CC into an orbital, then queue up a tank to anchor the early defense. The gas investment here sets up mineral flexibility for the heavier tech to follow.

[Step 4] Keep SCVs rolling to saturate the economy while adding 2 Refineries to strengthen gas income for the tech transition. Attach a tech lab to the factory and start a starport, then queue up a tank as the factory tech completes. Train a marine alongside the worker production to keep modest production tempo and fill out supply without overcommitting minerals.

[Step 5] Keep SCV production rolling to push worker saturation while adding a tank from the factory for defensive backbone. Train a marine and throw down a depot to keep supply headroom healthy for the ongoing production cycle. Add a tech lab to the starport to open up gas-heavy air options, and drop a bunker to lock down a key position as the economy scales up.

[Step 6] Add another rax and throw a tech lab on it to unlock stim, which you'll want to start researching right away. Keep workers rolling to keep saturating the economy while the factory pushes out a tank. Grab the Corvid Reactor upgrade for your raven to get that energy regeneration online, and make sure you've got enough gas flowing to support this tech-heavy phase.

[Step 7] Keep several SCVs rolling to keep the economy developing while throwing down a couple of depots to stay ahead of supply for the tank and marine production. Build 2 Siege Tanks out of the factory to anchor the defensive posture, and train a Marine to round out the army. The gas-heavy tank production is chewing through vespene, so the mineral-to-gas balance should be watched as the tech scales up.

[Step 8] Add a raven from the starport while the factory churns out a mine and 2 tanks for a solid mech backbone. Keep marine production flowing and throw down 3 depots to stay ahead of supply as the army grows. The gas-heavy raven and tank production is stretching minerals and gas simultaneously, so the economy needs to keep pace on both fronts.

[Step 9] Keep marine production rolling from the rax while throwing down a couple depots to stay ahead of supply, then add a lib from the starport and mix in a tank plus a widow mine from the factory to build out a rounded composition. Use the gas income to support the heavier factory and starport units without cutting marine reinforcements.

[Step 10] Kick off combat shield research while the factory churns out a tank and a widow mine for defensive coverage. Drop an ebay to unlock future upgrades, then cycle the rax and starport to produce several marines and a liberator. Add another tank from the factory and throw down a depot to keep supply headroom healthy as bio and air production ramps up.

[Step 11] Keep marine production rolling while throwing down depots to stay ahead of supply, and add a widow mine and a couple of tanks from the factory for area denial. The starport kicks out a medivac to support the bio force, so make sure gas income can handle the heavy tank and medivac demand without stalling.

[Step 12] Push out a wave of Marines from the rax while the starport and factory kick into gear with a medivac, a tank, and a couple of mines. Keep an eye on supply headroom and throw down depots as needed so this production spike doesn't stall out, and let the gas flow support the heavier factory and starport outputs.

[Step 13] Keep marine production rolling with a wave of infantry while adding a lib and a couple of mines for map control and area denial. Throw down a depot to keep supply headroom comfortable for the ongoing unit cycle, letting gas income flex toward the port and factory output without straining the mineral economy.

[Step 14] Keep marine production rolling out of the rax while adding a tank from the factory and mixing in libs and a raven from the starport to round out the army composition. Make sure there's enough depot space for all this production without letting supply cap idle your structures. The gas draw from the tank, libs, and raven is hefty, so keep an eye on mineral and gas balance to keep queues running smooth.

[Step 15] This is a tight, gas-greedy bio-mech tempo build that leverages a two-base economy to spike an area-denial deathball of tanks, liberators, and ravens behind a marine screen. It plays like a classic mech siege comp with a bio frontline, aiming to choke the map with matrix support and sustained production rather than expand into a traditional macro game.
