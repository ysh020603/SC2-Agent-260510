# Summary

The opening goes depot into rax with an early Refinery, getting an orbital up on the home CC before taking an expansion and adding a Factory with a tech lab that transitions into a Starport, setting up a 2-base economy that churns out early Marines for map presence while starting the tank count. Production scales with 2 rax (1 with a tech lab for stim and combat shield), a Factory tech lab pumping tanks and mines, and a Starport tech lab producing Ravens, Liberators, and Medivacs off a Corvid Reactor grab, creating a bio-mech hybrid that leans on siege setups and air support for sustained pressure. The endgame aims to max out on a Marine-tank core with Liberator and Raven spellcaster backup, pushing for a 3-base economy that floods the map with widow mine drops and siege lines until the opponent cracks under relentless mixed aggression.
