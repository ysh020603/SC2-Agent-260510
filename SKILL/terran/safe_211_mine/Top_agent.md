# Summary

Open safely with a Reaper, Bunker, and natural expansion, then build a two-Barracks, one-Factory, one-Starport core. Produce Marines, a few Marauders, WidowMines, and Medivacs while researching Stimpack, CombatShield, and infantry weapons. Add SiegeTanks and Vikings as the game develops, take a third base after the first bio-mine force is ready, and attack once at least 36 Marines, four WidowMines, and two Medivacs are available.
