# Summary

The build opens with a standard rax-first into expand, grabbing gas early to support a Factory tech path that quickly pivots into double Factory tank production with siege mode while both CCs get morphed into orbitals for macro and scan utility. Mid-game the production scales hard with 3 rax added on top of the existing factories, mixing reactor and tech lab configurations to flood marines while factories crank out tanks, combat shield comes online, and the army plays as a bio-mech deathball that leverages marine mineral efficiency to screen for the gas-heavy tank core. Late-game the build pushes toward a 3-base economy with additional OCs for mule saturation, aiming to max out on a marine-tank composition that overwhelms through sustained production rather than tech switching, with depots constantly thrown down to keep supply from choking the macro cycle.

[Step 1] Keep workers rolling to saturate the main while throwing down a depot and a rax to open up production. Drop a Refinery to start building gas income for tech flexibility, then lay down another CC to get the expansion timing going.

[Step 2] Keep SCV production rolling while the economy still has room to saturate, throw down a depot to keep supply headroom healthy for the upcoming SCV and marine cycle, and add a Refinery to strengthen the gas economy for the upcoming tech path. Build a factory to move into mech production, and train a couple of marines for early map presence while the tech comes online.

[Step 3] Keep SCV production rolling with several workers coming out of the CC while throwing down two depots to keep supply headroom healthy for the upcoming production cycle. Add a factory tech lab to unlock gas-heavy options, pacing mineral and gas income for the tech path ahead.

[Step 4] Throw down a factory and immediately start a tank, signaling the shift into mech tech. Keep SCV production rolling as you morph both Command Centers into orbitals, locking in the economy and scan potential for the mid-game. The gas investment here sets up mineral flexibility for future production cycles while the fresh orbitals start generating energy for mules and map awareness.

[Step 5] Keep SCV production rolling while the economy still has room to saturate, and start a tank from the factory while adding a tech lab to unlock siege mode. Throw down a Refinery to strengthen the gas economy for upcoming tech flexibility, then add several depots to stay ahead of supply demand from the worker and production cycle without overcommitting minerals to unused cap.

[Step 6] Add a rax and build a tech lab on it, then grab another Refinery to strengthen gas flexibility for upcoming tech. Keep SCV production rolling to push worker saturation while the economy still has room to grow. Queue up a couple of tanks from the factory and kick off combat shield research, making sure to pace depot construction for the supply headroom needed by this SCV and tank cycle.

[Step 7] Keep marine production rolling from the rax while pumping out 4 tanks from the factory, building a solid bio-mech core. Throw down a depot to keep supply headroom healthy for the next wave of units without capping out. The gas flow here is supporting the heavy tank investment, so keep mineral income strong to balance the bio side.

[Step 8] Throw down 3 rax and a depot to open up production capacity, then add reactors to a couple of them to start cycling marines. Meanwhile, queue up 3 tanks from the factory to anchor the army, and reinforce with several marines as the rax come online. Keep an eye on supply headroom so the growing production doesn't get capped, and make sure the gas economy can support the tank-heavy output alongside the reactor investments.

[Step 9] Add a factory to pivot into tank production, then churn out a wave of marines from the rax while queuing up 2 tanks to anchor the army. Keep an eye on supply headroom as the marine count spikes, and make sure the gas economy can support the factory's heavier demands going forward.

[Step 10] Keep marine production rolling while adding a tank and building a factory tech lab to unlock siege mode. Throw down 3 depots to stay ahead of supply as production ramps up, keeping mineral and gas income flowing for the heavier gas investments coming online.

[Step 11] Throw down a depot to keep supply headroom healthy for the incoming wave of units. Queue up 3 tanks from the factory to start building out a mech backbone, and reinforce with a handful of marines to round out the army. The gas-heavy tank production and mineral-light marine mix should balance out well as long as the economy keeps pace.

[Step 12] Pump out a wave of Marines from the rax while queuing 2 Siege Tanks from the factory, giving you a solid mineral-light core to screen for the gas-heavy tank production. Keep an eye on supply headroom so the barracks and factory don't get gated by cap as this production cycle rolls through.

[Step 13] Take another CC to keep expanding the economy while a tank rolls out of the factory for siege support. Train a wave of 8 marines to reinforce the bio core and keep supply headroom healthy for the ongoing production cycle.

[Step 14] Morph an OC to unlock macro orbitals while dumping minerals into a wave of Marines and a Siege Tank, keeping the mineral-to-gas ratio tilted toward bio with just enough gas income to support tank production.

[Step 15] Dump minerals into a massive wave of marines across all available rax, burning through supply quickly while keeping an eye on depot headroom so production doesn't stall out.

[Step 16] This is a classic two-base bio-mech snowball that leverages a tight tank-heavy core backed by relentless marine flooding, playing as a mineral-efficient deathball that scales into a macro siege monster.
