# Summary

The build opens with a standard rax-first into quick CC expansion, grabbing early gas and transitioning into a Factory with tech lab to start Siege Tank production while keeping worker saturation rolling. Mid-game spikes hard with a massive 10-rax production floor backed by 2 Factories, running reactors on Barracks to flood Marines while the tech-labbed Factory cranks Tanks, plus picking up combat shields to toughen the bio backbone. The endgame aims for a chunky bio-mech deathball with constant reinforcement, expanding to 3 bases to feed the insane mineral dump into Marines while gas fuels steady Tank cycles, looking to overwhelm with endless unit waves rather than tech switching.
