# Summary

The build opens with a standard depot-first into rax, grabbing an early natural CC and a pair of refineries before laying down a factory to pivot into tank production, with the initial CC morphing to OC once the mineral line is established and a tech lab on the factory unlocking siege mode for the defensive mech backbone. The mid-game ramps hard with 3 rax (two on reactor, one on tech lab for combat shield) alongside a pair of factories pumping tanks, creating a bio-mech hybrid that leans on marine swarms to screen for the siege line while slowly grinding forward with tank count and depots thrown down aggressively to prevent supply blocks. The late-game aims to max out on a marine-tank deathball supported by 3+ bases worth of economy, with OCs spreading across the map for mule income and scan utility, eventually looking to hit a critical mass of tanks behind a bio buffer to siege into entrenched positions or defend all-ins with the stacked firepower of a heavy mech core.
