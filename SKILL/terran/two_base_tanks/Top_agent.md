# Summary

**Against Terran:** The build opens with a standard depot-first into rax, grabbing an early natural CC and a pair of refineries before laying down a factory to pivot into tank production, with the initial CC morphing to OC once the mineral line is established and a tech lab on the factory unlocking siege mode for the defensive mech backbone. The mid-game ramps hard with 3 rax (two on reactor, one on tech lab for combat shield) alongside a pair of factories pumping tanks, creating a bio-mech hybrid that leans on marine swarms to screen for the siege line while slowly grinding forward with tank count and depots thrown down aggressively to prevent supply blocks. The late-game aims to max out on a marine-tank deathball supported by 3+ bases worth of economy, with OCs spreading across the map for mule income and scan utility, eventually looking to hit a critical mass of tanks behind a bio buffer to siege into entrenched positions or defend all-ins with the stacked firepower of a heavy mech core.

**Against Protoss:** The opening is a standard rax-first into quick CC expand, grabbing early gas and a Factory to pivot into mech while floating both CCs to orbitals for MULE economy and scan utility. The mid-game spikes hard with 3 rax reactors and a pair of Factories pumping non-stop, layering combat shields onto a marine-tank core that plays a defensive, siege-heavy style with constant bio reinforcement flooding the field. The late-game pushes toward a 3-base economy supporting a maxed bio-mech deathball, aiming to drown opponents under endless marine waves backed by a critical mass of sieged tanks, with no real tech switch—just pure sustained production and overwhelming frontline pressure.

**Against Zerg:** The build opens with a standard rax-first into expand, grabbing gas early to support a Factory tech path that quickly pivots into double Factory tank production with siege mode while both CCs get morphed into orbitals for macro and scan utility. Mid-game the production scales hard with 3 rax added on top of the existing factories, mixing reactor and tech lab configurations to flood marines while factories crank out tanks, combat shield comes online, and the army plays as a bio-mech deathball that leverages marine mineral efficiency to screen for the gas-heavy tank core. Late-game the build pushes toward a 3-base economy with additional OCs for mule saturation, aiming to max out on a marine-tank composition that overwhelms through sustained production rather than tech switching, with depots constantly thrown down to keep supply from choking the macro cycle.
