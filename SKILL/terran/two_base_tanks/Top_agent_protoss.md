# Summary

The build opens with a standard rax-first into CC, grabbing gas early to unlock factory tech and dropping a tech lab to start the tank transition while keeping marine production rolling for early defense. The mid-game spikes hard with a sudden flood of 8 rax alongside continued factory output, morphing both CCs into orbitals to fund the MULE economy and mixing reactors with tech labs to pump marines while siege tanks anchor the firepower, all without grabbing stim but picking up combat shields to keep the bio momentum alive. The late-game pushes toward a massive bio-mech deathball, taking another CC to support the economy and drowning opponents in an endless cycle of marine waves backed by tank support, aiming to max out and overwhelm through sheer production volume rather than teching into higher-tier units.
