# Summary

The build opens with a depot-first into a quick rax and early Refinery, getting an OC up fast for mule energy while adding a reactor for double marine production and a factory to start the gas-heavy tech path; the natural CC goes down early to set up the expansion and a reaper scouts for map presence. From there the build scales into 3 rax with a mix of tech labs and reactors, adding a starport for medivac support while rushing stim and combat shield to power the bio core, pumping marines and marauders with tank and medivac backup for a classic MMM siege push that keeps constant pressure. The late game floods the map with a massive bio ball supported by vikings for air control, scaling up to 4+ bases with reactors everywhere and a heavy marine-marauder-medivac-tank composition that aims to overwhelm through sheer production volume and drop potential rather than tech switching.
