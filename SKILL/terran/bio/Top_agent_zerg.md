# Summary

The build opens with a standard depot-into-double-rax setup, grabbing an early reaper for map tempo before dropping the natural CC and transitioning into a Factory opening with a reactor on the rax; a bunker goes up for safety while the Factory tech lab unlocks the gas-heavy path and an OC morphs on the main to keep macro rolling. The mid-game scales hard into a 5-rax bio core with a Starport online, pushing stim and combat shields while swapping add-ons to get reactors flowing on multiple rax; the army floods marines, marauders, medivacs, and a viking for map control, with concussive shells adding utility as the production tempo ramps into sustained pressure. The late-game aims for a maxed-out bio-ball supported by a medivac pipeline and starport reactor, with the economy stretching across 3 bases and gas flowing through 4 refineries to keep the rax hot; the endgame plan is overwhelming sustained production with a heavy MMM core, using drop play and frontal pushes to grind down the opponent rather than tech-switching to mech or air.
