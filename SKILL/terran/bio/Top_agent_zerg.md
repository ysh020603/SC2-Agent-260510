# Summary

The build opens with a standard rax-first into OC, grabbing early gas and adding a reactor for marine production before dropping a factory with a tech lab to unlock tanks, signaling a bio-mech hybrid setup that quickly pivots toward full bio once the starport comes online. Mid-game hits with a 5-rax production backbone mixing tech labs and reactors, pumping marines and marauders while the starport churns medivacs and a viking for air presence, with stim, combat shields, and concussive shells all ticking away to power a classic MMM ball supported by siege tank anchors. The endgame aims for a maxed-out bio deathball with medivac drop play and sustained multi-prong pressure, floating a 3-base economy with ample gas from 4 refineries to keep the rax and starport humming, while the extra orbitals feed scan and mule greed to push the macro advantage into an overwhelming unit flood.
