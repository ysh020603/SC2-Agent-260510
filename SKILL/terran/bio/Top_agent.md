# Summary

**Against Terran:** This build opens with a standard depot-first into rax opener, getting gas early and adding a pair of barracks before taking the natural expansion and morphing both CCs into orbitals for mule economy; the tech path pivots through factory with a tech lab into starport, setting up the classic bio-mech backbone with tank and medivac production while keeping marine pump active. The mid-game scales hard on rax count, slamming down stim and combat shields while spreading reactors across multiple barracks to flood marines and mixing in marauders from tech labs, with the starport spitting medivacs and the occasional viking for air coverage—this is a macro-oriented, sustained pressure style that keeps units rolling across the map rather than looking for one big all-in. By the late game the economy supports 3+ bases with a massive bio core backed by tank siege support and a medivac cloud for drops and pickups, aiming to max out on a marine-marauder-tank-medivac deathball that can trade efficiently and remax instantly off a huge production backbone, with no real tech switch—just pure relentless bio-mech macro until the opponent drowns.

**Against Protoss:** The build opens with a depot-first into a quick rax and early Refinery, getting an OC up fast for mule energy while adding a reactor for double marine production and a factory to start the gas-heavy tech path; the natural CC goes down early to set up the expansion and a reaper scouts for map presence. From there the build scales into 3 rax with a mix of tech labs and reactors, adding a starport for medivac support while rushing stim and combat shield to power the bio core, pumping marines and marauders with tank and medivac backup for a classic MMM siege push that keeps constant pressure. The late game floods the map with a massive bio ball supported by vikings for air control, scaling up to 4+ bases with reactors everywhere and a heavy marine-marauder-medivac-tank composition that aims to overwhelm through sheer production volume and drop potential rather than tech switching.

**Against Zerg:** The build opens with a standard rax-first into OC, grabbing early gas and adding a reactor for marine production before dropping a factory with a tech lab to unlock tanks, signaling a bio-mech hybrid setup that quickly pivots toward full bio once the starport comes online. Mid-game hits with a 5-rax production backbone mixing tech labs and reactors, pumping marines and marauders while the starport churns medivacs and a viking for air presence, with stim, combat shields, and concussive shells all ticking away to power a classic MMM ball supported by siege tank anchors. The endgame aims for a maxed-out bio deathball with medivac drop play and sustained multi-prong pressure, floating a 3-base economy with ample gas from 4 refineries to keep the rax and starport humming, while the extra orbitals feed scan and mule greed to push the macro advantage into an overwhelming unit flood.
