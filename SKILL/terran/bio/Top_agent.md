# Summary

**Against Terran:** This build opens with a standard depot-first into rax opener, getting gas early and adding a pair of barracks before taking the natural expansion and morphing both CCs into orbitals for mule economy; the tech path pivots through FactoryTechLab into Starport, setting up the classic bio-mech backbone with tank and medivac production while keeping marine pump active. The mid-game scales hard on rax count, slamming down Stimpack and ShieldWall while spreading BarracksReactors across multiple Barracks to flood Marines and mixing in Marauders from BarracksTechLabs, with the Starport producing Medivacs and the occasional VikingFighter for air coverage—this is a macro-oriented, sustained pressure style that keeps units rolling across the map rather than looking for one big all-in. By the late game the economy supports 3+ bases with a massive bio core backed by SiegeTank support and a Medivac cloud for drops and pickups, aiming to max out on a Marine-Marauder-SiegeTank-Medivac deathball that can trade efficiently and remax instantly off a huge production backbone, with no real tech switch—just pure relentless bio-mech macro until the opponent drowns.

**Against Protoss:** The build opens with a depot-first into a quick Barracks and early Refinery, getting an OrbitalCommand up fast for mule energy while adding BarracksReactor for double Marine production and a Factory to start the gas-heavy tech path; the natural CommandCenter goes down early to set up the expansion and a Reaper scouts for map presence. From there the build scales into three Barracks with a mix of BarracksTechLab and BarracksReactor, adding a Starport for Medivac support while rushing Stimpack and ShieldWall to power the bio core, pumping Marines and Marauders with SiegeTank and Medivac backup for a classic MMM siege push that keeps constant pressure. The late game floods the map with a massive bio ball supported by VikingFighters for air control, scaling up to 4+ bases with BarracksReactors and a heavy Marine-Marauder-Medivac-SiegeTank composition that aims to overwhelm through sheer production volume and drop potential rather than tech switching.

**Against Zerg:** The build opens with a standard Barracks-first into OrbitalCommand, grabbing early gas and adding BarracksReactor for Marine production before dropping a Factory with FactoryTechLab to unlock SiegeTanks, signaling a bio-mech hybrid setup that quickly pivots toward full bio once the Starport comes online. Mid-game hits with a five-Barracks production backbone mixing BarracksTechLabs and BarracksReactors, pumping Marines and Marauders while the Starport churns Medivacs and a VikingFighter for air presence, with Stimpack, ShieldWall, and PunisherGrenades all ticking away to power a classic MMM ball supported by SiegeTank anchors. The endgame aims for a maxed-out bio deathball with Medivac drop play and sustained multi-prong pressure, floating a three-base economy with ample gas from four copies of Refinery to keep Barracks and Starport humming, while extra OrbitalCommands feed scan and mule economy to push the macro advantage into an overwhelming unit flood.
