# Summary

This build opens with a standard depot-first into rax opener, getting gas early and adding a pair of barracks before taking the natural expansion and morphing both CCs into orbitals for mule economy; the tech path pivots through factory with a tech lab into starport, setting up the classic bio-mech backbone with tank and medivac production while keeping marine pump active. The mid-game scales hard on rax count, slamming down stim and combat shields while spreading reactors across multiple barracks to flood marines and mixing in marauders from tech labs, with the starport spitting medivacs and the occasional viking for air coverage—this is a macro-oriented, sustained pressure style that keeps units rolling across the map rather than looking for one big all-in. By the late game the economy supports 3+ bases with a massive bio core backed by tank siege support and a medivac cloud for drops and pickups, aiming to max out on a marine-marauder-tank-medivac deathball that can trade efficiently and remax instantly off a huge production backbone, with no real tech switch—just pure relentless bio-mech macro until the opponent drowns.
