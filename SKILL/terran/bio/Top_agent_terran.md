# Summary

Rusty Infantry opens with a reaper-expand into 2 rax before laying down a factory and starport, getting an early orbital and tech-labbing the factory while stabilizing with marine production and concussive shells. The mid-game scales into a 5+ rax bio core with stim and combat shield, mixing in siege tanks from the factory and medivacs/vikings from the starport to build a bio-mech army that applies sustained pressure while expanding across 3 bases. The late-game floods marauders and marines with reactor swaps on multiple rax, sustaining a chunky bio ball supported by tank and viking production off a 4-base economy, aiming to max out and overwhelm with constant unit cycles rather than tech-switching.
