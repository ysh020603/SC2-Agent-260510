# Summary

**Against Terran:** This build order executes a fast, aggressive bio timing push centered on early Marauders backed by Marines, using a quick double Barracks opening with a Reaper scout and fast Orbital Commands to fuel constant production. The early game features a standard expand (first CC at step 2) with gas-heavy tech: Tech Lab on the first Barracks for Punisher Grenades and Marauder production, a Reactor on the second Barracks for Marine output, and ShieldWall researched to boost Marauder survivability. The tech path remains purely Barracks-based with no Factory or Starport, focusing on Marine-Marauder composition throughout. After building a solid early army and taking a natural expansion at step 9, the build transitions into mass Marine production by attaching Reactors to additional Barracks and churning out waves of Marines, aiming to overwhelm the opponent with sustained bio pressure and map control.
