# Summary

The build opens with a standard rax-first into quick expand, grabbing early gas and using a reaper for scout and map presence before transitioning into a Factory tech path with a Starport follow-up to unlock air options. Production scales through a 1-1-1 core that quickly blossoms into multi-port Liberator and Viking production, with Reactors and Tech Labs spread across rax, Factory, and Starports to enable mixed bio-mech output; the mid-game leans on Raven support, Siege Tank control, and a sudden spike of air units to establish map pressure, while an EBay kicks off +1 attack and an Armory opens mech upgrades as the economy explodes across multiple CCs. The late-game direction commits to a sky-terran-inflected bio-mech deathball, dumping gas aggressively into Liberator and Viking fleets supported by marine-tank ground presence, with the sprawling multi-base economy and heavy Refinery count fueling sustained production aimed at overwhelming through air dominance and siege-position control rather than a narrow timing push.
