# Summary

The build opens with a standard depot-into-rax setup, grabbing an early reaper for scout and tempo while the CC flips to an orbital, then throws down a reactor and floods out 4 additional rax before taking a natural CC to set up a heavy bio macro shell. Production scales hard with a 5-rax core mixing tech labs and reactors, kicking off stim and combat shields to get the bio ball rolling, then layering in a factory and starport for gas flexibility while spamming marines and keeping depot pace tight to avoid supply blocks. The endgame aims for a maxed-out bio flood backed by eventual air support from the starport, running a 2-base economy with triple orbital mule income and concussive shells online, looking to overwhelm with sustained marine production and tech flexibility rather than a sharp timing.
