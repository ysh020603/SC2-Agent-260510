# Summary

The build opens with a standard rax-first into expand, getting gas early for flexibility and dropping a Reaper for initial scout pressure before quickly transitioning into a heavy 5-rax marine flood with mixed tech labs and reactors. The midgame is all about cranking out marines non-stop while layering stim, combat shields, and concussive shells to power up the bio ball, staying ahead of supply with constant depots and eventually adding a factory and starport to open up tech options. The late game aims to leverage a saturated 2-base economy into overwhelming marine production, with the starport and factory providing a bridge toward medivac support or heavier mech transitions if the bio flood doesn't break the opponent outright.

# Details

[Step 1] Keep SCV production rolling to saturate the main while throwing down a rax and a Refinery to open up gas flexibility for tech. Drop a couple of depots to stay ahead of supply as production ramps up, then queue a reaper for early map presence.

[Step 2] Morph the CC into an orbital and keep SCV production rolling while the economy still has room to grow. Throw down a reactor on the existing rax, then add 4 fresh rax to explode bio production capacity. Drop a Refinery to strengthen the gas economy for upcoming tech flexibility, and stay on top of depot timing as this production surge will chew through supply quickly.

[Step 3] Keep a few SCVs in production while adding a tech lab and reactor across the rax to unlock flexible output, then queue up several Marines for early map presence. Throw down a couple depots to stay ahead of the supply curve as production ramps, and kick off stim research to power up bio for the midgame. The tech lab investment starts pulling gas, so mineral income stays directed toward units and infrastructure while gas builds toward key upgrades.

[Step 4] Throw down depots as needed to keep supply headroom ahead of the marine production curve, while running a wave of marines out of the rax to build up the bio ball. Keep the mineral pipeline flowing into both depots and marines so nothing stalls out.

[Step 5] Take an expansion while keeping SCV production rolling to pace worker saturation across both bases. Throw down a depot to buffer supply for the marine and SCV cycle, then queue up several Marines to reinforce the army.

[Step 6] Keep SCV production rolling to saturate the economy while churning out a wave of Marines from the rax, making sure to pace depot construction ahead of the supply curve so the bio flood doesn't get supply-blocked.

[Step 7] Keep marine production rolling as you morph an orbital and start combat shield research, which sets up a stronger bio core. Throw down a depot to keep supply headroom healthy through the marine and SCV cycle, and pace a few more workers while the economy task shifts toward OC energy usage. The gas going into shields tightens mineral flexibility, so keep an eye on macro balance for the next tech or production wave.

[Step 8] Keep SCV production ticking to push worker saturation while the economy develops, and reinforce with a wave of Marines from the raxes to build up the bio core. Watch the supply cap and throw down depots as needed so the constant marine and SCV flow doesn't get blocked.

[Step 9] Keep SCV production rolling to push worker saturation while the economy still has room to grow, and throw down a depot soon to buffer the supply headroom from this heavy marine and SCV cycle. Train a wave of marines from the rax to build up a solid bio force, making sure not to overcommit minerals to unused supply cap.

[Step 10] Pump out a wave of Marines from the rax while throwing down a depot to keep supply headroom ahead of the production cycle. Grab Concussive Shells to give bio some punch against mobile targets, then drop a Factory to open up gas-heavy tech options down the line.

[Step 11] Train a wave of Marines to reinforce the bio ball, then throw down a depot to keep supply headroom comfortable for the continued marine production without stalling out.

[Step 12] Throw down a couple of depots to keep supply headroom ahead of the marine rally, then churn out a wave of marines while the rax stays active. Drop a starport to open up the gas-heavy air tech path, and keep mineral flow going into more marines as the port comes online.

[Step 13] This is a classic bio-macro snowball that floods the map with marines off a saturated two-base economy, layering stim and shield upgrades to create an overwhelming midgame bio ball before bridging into factory-starport tech. It plays as a relentless pressure build that forces the opponent to react to constant marine production while keeping the door open for medivac or mech transitions.
