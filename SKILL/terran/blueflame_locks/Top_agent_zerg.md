# Summary

The opening is a standard rax-first into quick expansion, getting both CCs onto orbitals fast while the factory with tech lab and reactor sets up the mech backbone, with early cyclones and hellions providing map presence and defense. Production scales hard into a 4-5 factory mech core mixing reactors and tech labs, flooding hellions and cyclones while grabbing blue flame, armory, and ebay upgrades, supported by starport tech for ravens and vikings as the macro-oriented style leans on sustained pressure and economic growth across multiple bases. The endgame pushes toward a heavy thor-hellion-cyclone deathball with viking support, maxing out on 4-5 bases with double armory upgrades and planetary fortifications locking down expansions, aiming to overwhelm with unrelenting mech production and upgrade advantage.
