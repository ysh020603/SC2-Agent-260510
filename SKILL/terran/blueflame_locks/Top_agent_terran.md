# Summary

The build opens with a standard depot-first into rax and natural CC, quickly grabbing gas before dropping a factory to kickstart the mech transition while keeping both orbitals churning out workers. Production scales hard into a 4-factory setup with tech labs and reactors, flooding hellions with blue flame and mixing in cyclones with Lock On upgrade, thors, and starport support from vikings and ravens while an ebay preps +1. The endgame aims for a maxed-out mech deathball with heavy hellion/cyclone/thor core backed by air support, running on a 4-base economy with planetary defenses and sustained factory production to overwhelm through attrition and map control.

# Details

[Step 1] Throw down a depot to keep supply headroom ahead of the SCV and production ramp-up, then keep several SCVs in production as you take an expansion and start a rax. Drop a Refinery to build out gas flexibility for whatever tech direction you want to take next.

[Step 2] Keep SCV production rolling while throwing down depots to build a comfortable supply buffer for upcoming production cycles, then morph both CCs into orbitals to unlock macro mechanics and prepare for the midgame economy, rounding out with a marine to start building a small defense force.

[Step 3] Throw down a factory to kickstart the tech transition while keeping worker production rolling to push economy saturation. Add a Refinery to open up gas flexibility for whatever factory tech path you want to pursue. Train a couple of Marines on the side for map presence and to keep the rax busy, and make sure you've got depot space lined up for the next SCV and Marine cycle without letting supply cap get tight.

[Step 4] Keep SCV production rolling to saturate the economy while taking another CC and adding a factory with a tech lab. Throw down two Refineries to strengthen gas income for the upcoming tech path, and queue up another marine to keep modest production flowing.

[Step 5] Keep workers rolling as the economy still has room to saturate, while building 2 cyclones and adding a reactor to the factory for faster production. Pick up the Cyclone Lock On damage upgrade to make those cyclones hit harder, and make sure there's enough gas income flowing to support this tech-heavy phase.

[Step 6] Throw down a factory and keep worker production rolling to pace saturation alongside the fresh orbital. Take a couple of refineries to strengthen gas income for the tech transition, then queue up a cyclone and a pair of hellions while adding a factory tech lab to unlock more advanced options.

[Step 7] Throw down an armory to unlock higher-tier upgrades while keeping SCV production rolling to push worker saturation forward. Train a cyclone out of the factory for early map presence and mobile defense, then grab blue flame to give hellions serious punch against light targets. Keep an eye on depot timing as the production cycle picks up, and make sure gas income stays healthy to feed the armory and vehicle tech path.

[Step 8] Keep SCV production rolling to saturate the economy while throwing down depots to stay ahead of supply as production ramps up. The steady worker flow and depot spacing should keep minerals moving without hitting cap or leaving too much unused supply room.

[Step 9] Keep workers pumping to push saturation while churning out 2 Cyclones from the factory, then add another factory to open more production lines. Round out the army with a couple of Hellions and keep SCV production rolling as the economy expands.

[Step 10] Throw down a factory and immediately attach a tech lab to unlock cyclone production, then queue up a couple of cyclones alongside several hellions to build a mobile mobile map presence force. Drop 3 depots to keep supply headroom ahead of this production spike, ensuring the gas-heavy cyclone count doesn't stall out the mineral flow needed for continued macro.

[Step 11] Keep factory production rolling with a couple of Hellions and Cyclones, throw down 3 depots to keep supply headroom healthy for the upcoming unit cycle, and add a reactor to the factory to double Hellion output. Drop an ebay to prep for upgrades, and take another CC to keep the economy growing as the gas-heavy tech demands more mineral and gas income.

[Step 12] Throw down a starport to open up air options while the factory keeps busy with a cyclone and a wave of hellions. Drop 4 depots to keep supply headroom well ahead of the production spike, ensuring you don't get blocked as the factory and starport come online. The gas is starting to flow heavier now, supporting both the cyclone and future port tech without tying every geyser to a specific unit.

[Step 13] Queue up a Thor from the factory to anchor your mech core, then flood out a wave of Hellions for map control and mineral sink. Add a tech lab to the starport to unlock port tech options. Keep an eye on supply headroom as this production batch chews through cap, and lean on your gas income to sustain the Thor alongside the lighter mineral commitments of the Hellion swarm.

[Step 14] Queue a raven to support the army while the factory pumps out a wave of hellions and a pair of thors for heavy firepower, morph a planetary to lock down an expansion, add another refinery to strengthen gas flexibility for upcoming tech, and build an additional factory to scale production.

[Step 15] Drop a factory and add another Refinery to strengthen gas flexibility for the tech transition, then crank out a wave of Hellions alongside a Thor and a Raven for a powerful mech opener. Keep SCV production rolling to maintain saturation while making sure depot space keeps pace with this heavy production cycle.

[Step 16] Push out a wave of Hellions from the factories to establish map presence and light mobile map presence potential, while adding tech labs to each factory to unlock more versatile production. Drop an armory to set up the transition into heavier mech upgrades and advanced units, and slip in a marine to keep the rax active without stalling the build. Keep an eye on supply headroom as this Hellion-heavy step eats into cap quickly, and ensure the gas economy can support the upcoming tech lab and armory demands.

[Step 17] Push factory production hard with a mix of 2 Cyclones, 5 Hellions, and 2 Thors while the starport churns out 2 Vikings and a Raven for air control and support. Keep a few Marines training from the rax to round out the army, and make sure depot construction keeps pace with this heavy production cycle so you don't get supply blocked. The gas investment here is substantial, so ensure your refineries are keeping the income flowing for these mech and air units.

[Step 18] This is a classic mech macro snowball that floods the map with blue-flame hellions and lock-on cyclones before anchoring into a thor-heavy deathball, leaning on relentless factory production and incremental air support to choke out opponents through attrition and map control. Use this gameplan as your strategic baseline -- adapt your decisions based on what you scout and how the game unfolds.
