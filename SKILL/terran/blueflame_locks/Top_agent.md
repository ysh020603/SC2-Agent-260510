# Summary

**Against Terran:** The build opens with a standard depot-first into rax and natural CC, quickly grabbing gas before dropping a factory to kickstart the mech transition while keeping both orbitals churning out workers. Production scales hard into a 4-factory setup with tech labs and reactors, flooding hellions with blue flame and mixing in cyclones with Lock On upgrade, thors, and starport support from vikings and ravens while an ebay preps +1. The endgame aims for a maxed-out mech deathball with heavy hellion/cyclone/thor core backed by air support, running on a 4-base economy with planetary defenses and sustained factory production to overwhelm through attrition and map control.

**Against Protoss:** This build opens with a standard macro foundation—depot into CC and rax before grabbing gas and transitioning into a fast Factory with a tech lab, immediately prioritizing cyclone production and the lock-on damage upgrade while keeping worker production rolling to support the economic backbone. The mid-game scales hard into a mech-heavy setup with multiple factories running tech labs and reactors, flooding hellions alongside cyclones and thors while picking up blue flame, adding an armory for upgrades, and eventually a starport with a tech lab for raven and viking support to establish mobile map control and gas-heavy sustained pressure. The late-game aims to max out on a hellion-thor-cyclone core with raven support, pushing the economy toward 4+ bases and leveraging the armory and factory count to overwhelm through sheer production volume and upgrade-backed mech deathball rather than any sharp timing or air transition.

**Against Zerg:** The opening is a standard rax-first into quick expansion, getting both CCs onto orbitals fast while the factory with tech lab and reactor sets up the mech backbone, with early cyclones and hellions providing map presence and defense. Production scales hard into a 4-5 factory mech core mixing reactors and tech labs, flooding hellions and cyclones while grabbing blue flame, armory, and ebay upgrades, supported by starport tech for ravens and vikings as the macro-oriented style leans on sustained pressure and economic growth across multiple bases. The endgame pushes toward a heavy thor-hellion-cyclone deathball with viking support, maxing out on 4-5 bases with double armory upgrades and planetary fortifications locking down expansions, aiming to overwhelm with unrelenting mech production and upgrade advantage.
