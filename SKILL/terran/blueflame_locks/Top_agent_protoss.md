# Summary

This build opens with a standard macro foundation—depot into CC and rax before grabbing gas and transitioning into a fast Factory with a tech lab, immediately prioritizing cyclone production and the lock-on damage upgrade while keeping worker production rolling to support the economic backbone. The mid-game scales hard into a mech-heavy setup with multiple factories running tech labs and reactors, flooding hellions alongside cyclones and thors while picking up blue flame, adding an armory for upgrades, and eventually a starport with a tech lab for raven and viking support to establish mobile map control and gas-heavy sustained pressure. The late-game aims to max out on a hellion-thor-cyclone core with raven support, pushing the economy toward 4+ bases and leveraging the armory and factory count to overwhelm through sheer production volume and upgrade-backed mech deathball rather than any sharp timing or air transition.

# Details

[Step 1] Throw down a depot to keep supply headroom comfortable, then keep SCV production rolling with several workers as the economy still has room to saturate. Drop another CC to set up the expansion timing, add a rax to open up infantry production, and build a Refinery to strengthen gas income for whatever tech path follows.

[Step 2] Keep training SCVs while throwing down several depots to build a sturdy supply buffer for upcoming production. Morph both CCs into orbitals to unlock MULEs and vision options, then add a marine to start building a small defense force.

[Step 3] Throw down a factory to open up the tech path while keeping SCV production rolling to push worker saturation. Add another Refinery to strengthen gas income for the upcoming factory units and potential upgrades. Train a couple of Marines to maintain light map presence, and keep an eye on supply headroom with depots as the production cycle fills out.

[Step 4] Keep SCV production rolling to saturate the economy while taking another CC for future expansion. Add a factory with a tech lab to open up the mech tech path, and drop 2 Refineries to strengthen gas income for the upcoming production demands. Train a marine to keep modest map presence as the tech infrastructure comes online.

[Step 5] Keep worker production rolling to push saturation while the factory churns out a couple of cyclones. Attach a reactor to the factory for faster cyclone output, and pick up the lock-on damage upgrade to make those cyclones hit harder. Make sure you've got enough gas flowing to support this tech-heavy phase without stalling the build.

[Step 6] Keep worker production rolling as the economy develops, then throw down a factory and a Refinery to strengthen gas income for the upcoming tech path. Morph the CC into an orbital while training a Cyclone out of the factory, giving you some early map presence and defense as the build continues to scale.

[Step 7] Keep several SCVs rolling to saturate the economy while adding another Refinery to open up gas flexibility for tech transitions. Produce a couple of Hellions from the factory and attach a tech lab to it, then throw down an Armory to unlock future upgrades and heavier mech options.

[Step 8] Keep SCV production rolling to keep the economy growing while the new base or orbital comes online. Add a hellion and a cyclone to the army, then throw down 3 depots to stay ahead of the supply curve as production ramps up. The cyclone's gas cost means you'll want that refinery income flowing, so pace the factory units alongside worker saturation without stalling mineral spending.

[Step 9] Keep SCV production rolling as the economy still has room to saturate, while throwing down 2 depots to keep supply headroom ahead of the worker and production cycle. Grab blue flame to unlock hellion aggression and strengthen gas flexibility for upcoming tech.

[Step 10] Keep SCV production rolling to push worker saturation while the economy still has room to grow, and add another factory to expand the mech production backbone. Train 2 cyclones alongside a hellion for the opening skirmish force, making sure to pace depot construction so the supply headroom stays comfortable with all this production coming online. The gas income should support this heavier factory commitment without choking the mineral flow needed for workers and infrastructure.

[Step 11] Add a factory and immediately start pumping out a mix of hellions and cyclones to build a mobile ground force. Throw down a couple of depots to keep supply headroom healthy as production ramps up, then add a tech lab and reactor to the factory to unlock flexible unit options and double up production bandwidth. The gas investment here is substantial, so the mineral-to-gas balance should support the cyclone-heavy output without stalling the build.

[Step 12] Throw down an ebay and take another CC to keep the economy scaling while adding a starport for air tech. Produce a couple of Hellions alongside 2 Cyclones from the factory, and drop 3 depots to keep supply headroom healthy as production ramps up. The gas-heavy factory and starport investments mean you'll want that refinery income flowing smoothly for the upcoming tech transitions.

[Step 13] Push out a cyclone alongside a swarm of hellions from the factory while throwing down 4 depots to keep supply well ahead of the production spike. Drop a tech lab on the starport to open up gas-heavy air options as the mineral-to-gas ratio shifts with this heavy factory output.

[Step 14] Flood the factory with a wave of Hellions to build a mobile mobile map presence force, then pivot into heavier gas spending by queuing a Raven and a pair of Thors. Keep an eye on supply headroom so the depot buffer doesn't choke this production spike.

[Step 15] Morph a PF to secure the natural while adding 2 Refineries to strengthen gas flexibility for the heavy mech transition. Throw down 2 Factories and immediately start a Thor, then follow up with a wave of Hellions to map control and vision. Keep worker production rolling to backfill the economy as the new base comes online and production ramps up.

[Step 16] Produce a raven from the starport alongside several hellions flooding out of the factory to establish map presence, add a cyclone for armored target coverage, and attach tech labs to factories to unlock higher-tier factory options and strengthen the gas-heavy production pipeline going forward.

[Step 17] Throw down an armory to unlock upgrades and heavier mech options while the factory churns out a mix of hellions and 3 thors. Keep the starport busy with a couple of vikings for air coverage, and round out the production with a cyclone and a few more hellions to maintain map presence. Make sure mineral and gas income can support this gas-heavy army composition without stalling out.

[Step 18] This is a macro mech deathball that scales from early cyclone pressure into a relentless hellion-thor flood, leaning on production volume and armory upgrades to snowball into an overwhelming late-game ground force. Use this gameplan as your strategic baseline -- adapt your decisions based on what you scout and how the game unfolds.
