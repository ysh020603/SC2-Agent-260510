# Summary

The opening goes rax into quick natural CC with an orbital morph, following up with a factory and starport to set up mech tech while dropping a bunker for safety and keeping marine production rolling. Production scales with multiple rax, a reactor starport, and tech labs unlocking stim and combat shield as the army pivots to a marine-tank core supported by viking and BC production, with an armory and ebay powering upgrades alongside ship weapons for the air fleet. The endgame aims for a maxed-out sky-terran deathball with a heavy BC-viking fleet backed by marine-tank ground control, sustaining production across 3+ bases with planetary defense and turret coverage to secure the economy while yamato cannon and air upgrades let the rust fleet trade up and steamroll into the late game.
