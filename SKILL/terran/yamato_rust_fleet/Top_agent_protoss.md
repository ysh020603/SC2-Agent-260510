# Summary

The build opens with a standard rax-first into expand, grabbing gas early and flowing naturally into Factory -> Starport with a Fusion Core tacked on to unlock the BC tech path, while a bunker and some marines hold the fort. Production scales through 3 rax with mixed add-ons, a Factory pumping tanks, and multiple Starports with tech labs and reactors to feed a marine-tank core that quickly pivots into heavy air; key upgrades hit in the form of Yamato, Combat Shield, and Ship Weapons as the gas economy ramps and the player plays a defensive macro style with turrets and a Planetary to buy space. The endgame balloon payment is a maxed-out BC-Viking deathball supported by a sprawling 4+ base economy and marine filler, leaning on overwhelming capital ship production and upgrade advantage to brute force the win through sustained air dominance.
