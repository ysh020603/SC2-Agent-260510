# Summary

**Against Terran:** The opening goes rax into quick natural CC with an orbital morph, following up with a factory and starport to set up mech tech while dropping a bunker for safety and keeping marine production rolling. Production scales with multiple rax, a reactor starport, and specific production add-ons unlocking Stimpack and ShieldWall as the army pivots to a marine-tank core supported by viking and BC production, with an armory and ebay powering upgrades alongside ship weapons for the air fleet. The endgame aims for a maxed-out sky-terran deathball with a heavy BC-viking fleet backed by marine-tank ground control, sustaining production across 3+ bases with planetary defense and turret coverage to secure the economy while yamato cannon and air upgrades let the rust fleet trade up and steamroll into the late game.

**Against Protoss:** The build opens with a standard rax-first into expand, grabbing gas early and flowing naturally into Factory -> Starport with a Fusion Core tacked on to unlock the BC tech path, while a bunker and some marines hold the fort. Production scales through 3 rax with mixed add-ons, a Factory pumping tanks, and multiple Starports with specific TechLab/Reactor variants to feed a marine-tank core that quickly pivots into heavy air; key upgrades hit in the form of Yamato, ShieldWall, and Ship Weapons as the gas economy ramps and the player plays a defensive macro style with turrets and a Planetary to buy space. The endgame balloon payment is a maxed-out BC-Viking deathball supported by a sprawling 4+ base economy and marine filler, leaning on overwhelming capital ship production and upgrade advantage to brute force the win through sustained air dominance.

**Against Zerg:** The build opens with a standard rax-first into expand, grabbing early gas and setting up a Factory before pivoting hard into Fusion Core and Starport tech to unlock the Battlecruiser transition. Production scales through multiple rax and a Starport with a reactor switch, mixing marine-tank bio with early BCs while picking up ShieldWall, ship weapons, and Yamato Cannon to power the mid-game. The endgame aims for a maxed-out Battlecruiser fleet supported by Vikings and a marine bio core, bankrolling the gas-heavy air armada off 4+ bases with Planetary Fortress defense and sustained Starport production to overwhelm with beefy capital ships.
