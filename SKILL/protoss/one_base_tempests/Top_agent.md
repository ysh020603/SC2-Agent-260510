# Summary

Remain on one Nexus long enough to reach Stargate and FleetBeacon quickly through Pylon, Gateway, Assimilators, and CyberneticsCore. Use a small Gateway force for survival, keep supply open, and invest the gas bank into repeated Tempest production with relevant ProtossAirWeapons upgrades. The strategy depends on range and concentrated fire rather than a large economy; add a second Nexus only when the Tempest force has stabilized the map or the opponent refuses to attack.
