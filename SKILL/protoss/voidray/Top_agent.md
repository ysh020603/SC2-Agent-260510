# Summary

Build a safe Gateway opening with one CyberneticsCore, then establish one Stargate and a second Nexus before scaling air production. Use one or two Gateways, a ShieldBattery when pressured, and a small Zealot/Stalker screen to survive while the first VoidRays complete. Keep both mineral lines producing Probes, maintain Pylon power, and add gas only when a completed base has a free geyser.

Produce VoidRays as the main force and reach a first mobile group of four to six before taking unnecessary side technology. Add a second Stargate only when two-base income can sustain both, research ProtossAirWeapons from the existing CyberneticsCore, and build exactly one FleetBeacon for VoidRaySpeedUpgrade and later fleet scaling. Do not duplicate CyberneticsCores or FleetBeacons.

Attack exposed production and expansions while avoiding concentrated anti-air. If the enemy masses light anti-air, add a limited number of Zealots or Colossi rather than abandoning the fleet; if air control is secure, take a third Nexus and scale to three Stargates. Spend excess minerals on Probes, Pylons, expansion, and the ground screen while reserving gas for continuous VoidRay production and air upgrades.
