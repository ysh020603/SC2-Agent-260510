# Summary

Open with Probe and Pylon discipline, then Gateway, Assimilator, CyberneticsCore, and a second Gateway while keeping the one-base economy lean. Research WarpGateResearch and AdeptPiercingAttack, add enough Pylons to maintain powered production, and produce Adepts continuously for an early timing. Use mobility to attack mineral lines and isolated units; if the first wave does not end the game, add Nexus and TwilightCouncil-based upgrades rather than remaining on an exhausted one-base all-in.
