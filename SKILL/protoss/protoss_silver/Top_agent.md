# Summary

Use a simple two-base Protoss macro plan: continuous Probes, timely Pylons, Gateway, Assimilator, CyberneticsCore, Nexus, then additional Gateways and RoboticsFacility. Produce a balanced Zealot/Stalker/Immortal force, research WarpGateResearch and basic ground upgrades, and add Observers for scouting and detection. Prefer full saturation, safe expansion, and uninterrupted production over specialized all-ins, attacking after the army has a clear numerical advantage.
