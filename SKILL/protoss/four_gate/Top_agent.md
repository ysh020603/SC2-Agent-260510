# Summary

Open with Pylon, Gateway, Assimilator, and one CyberneticsCore. Put WarpGateResearch ahead of Stalkers, extra gas spending, and BlinkTech; the automation assigns Chrono Boost. Stop at exactly four production Gateways before the first attack; do not add duplicate CyberneticsCores, RoboticsBays, or more Gateways merely because minerals are available. Maintain Probe production and place each Pylon early enough to keep every Gateway powered.

After WarpGateResearch completes, the automation converts completed Gateways; never request WarpGate as a macro task. Build a first pressure force of roughly six to ten Gateway units, primarily Stalkers with a small Zealot or Sentry screen. Keep producing reinforcements before adding unrelated technology; the automated attack planner launches and controls the pressure once its threshold is met.

If the first pressure does not end the game, add a second Nexus once the one-base mineral line approaches saturation, then choose one transition: Blink and upgrades against mobile ground armies, or a single RoboticsFacility with Observers and Immortals against armored or cloaked threats. Keep the original four WarpGates until two-base income can support more production.
