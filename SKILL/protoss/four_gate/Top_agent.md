# Summary

Open with Pylon, Gateway, Assimilator, and one CyberneticsCore. Put WarpGateResearch ahead of Stalkers, extra gas spending, and BlinkTech, and ChronoBoost it until complete. Stop at exactly four production Gateways before the first attack; do not add duplicate CyberneticsCores, RoboticsBays, or more Gateways merely because minerals are available. Maintain Probe production and place each Pylon early enough to keep every Gateway powered.

Convert all completed Gateways to WarpGates and build a first pressure force of roughly six to ten Gateway units, primarily Stalkers with a small Zealot or Sentry screen. Spend each synchronized warp-in cycle before adding unrelated technology, attack when the four-Gate force is assembled, and reinforce the same pressure rather than waiting for a perfect late-game army.

If the first pressure does not end the game, add a second Nexus once the one-base mineral line approaches saturation, then choose one transition: Blink and upgrades against mobile ground armies, or a single RoboticsFacility with Observers and Immortals against armored or cloaked threats. Keep the original four WarpGates until two-base income can support more production.
