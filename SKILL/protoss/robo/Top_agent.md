# Summary

Open with Pylon, Gateway, Assimilator, one CyberneticsCore, and a timely second Nexus, then add exactly one RoboticsFacility. Do not duplicate prerequisite technology buildings. Keep two to four powered Gateways for the supporting army, take additional Assimilators only as mineral-line saturation and Robotics production require them, and keep Probes and Pylons ahead of demand.

Produce an Observer early, then maintain Immortal production against Marauders, Roaches, Stalkers, or other armored ground units. Use Zealots as a mineral-heavy front line and Stalkers for general support; a practical mid-game mix is two to four Immortals behind a larger Gateway screen. Add one RoboticsBay only when Colossi are justified by dense light units or Disruptors by slow compact armies, rather than building the Bay automatically.

Expand when a mineral line reaches about sixteen workers or when the current bases can continuously fund production. Add Forge upgrades after unit production is stable, avoid floating more than roughly 600 minerals by adding units, Pylons, or a Nexus, and avoid adding Gateways that the current economy cannot continuously use.
