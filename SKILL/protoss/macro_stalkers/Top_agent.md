# Summary

Play a stable Gateway macro opening with early Nexus, Pylons, Assimilators, CyberneticsCore, and continuous Probe production. Add several Gateways, research WarpGateResearch and BlinkTech, and build a mobile Stalker-heavy army while scaling ground weapons and armor. Expand when current bases approach saturation, add RoboticsFacility and Observers for detection, and use Blink mobility to trade efficiently instead of committing into unfavorable defensive positions.
