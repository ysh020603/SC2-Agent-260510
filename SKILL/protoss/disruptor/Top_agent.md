# Summary

Establish a safe Nexus/Gateway/CyberneticsCore economy, then add RoboticsFacility and RoboticsBay with sufficient Assimilators. Use Stalkers and Immortals to hold the front while producing Observers for vision and Disruptors for decisive area damage. Research supporting ground weapons, armor, and mobility upgrades, keep Pylons ahead of supply, and avoid overproducing fragile Disruptors without a conventional army to screen for them.
