# Summary

Expand early and grow toward one Queen per base while establishing one SpawningPool, one RoachWarren, two to four copies of Extractor, and Lair before one HydraliskDen. Do not duplicate these technology buildings. Build enough early Roaches to survive pressure before spending on Hydralisk technology, and keep Overlords ahead of the larger two-supply units.

Use Roaches as the durable front line and Hydralisks as ranged and anti-air support. Start near a two-to-one Roach-to-Hydralisk ratio against ground armies, moving toward one-to-one when enemy air or low-health ranged units demand more Hydralisks. Research GlialReconstitution before a major Roach attack and both EvolveGroovedSpines and EvolveMuscularAugments before relying heavily on Hydralisks.

Use no more than two EvolutionChambers, one for missile weapons and one for armor, and do not rebuild them while existing copies are alive or under construction. Saturate three bases, inject every Hatchery, and turn a mineral bank above roughly 700 into Drones, Queens, Roaches, or an additional Hatchery instead of accumulating resources. Push after upgrades and a balanced reinforcement wave complete, then keep replacing the front line.
