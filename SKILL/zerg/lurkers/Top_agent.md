# Summary

Build a stable two-base Roach/Hydralisk economy before advancing through Lair, HydraliskDen, and LurkerDenMP. Use Roaches to absorb early pressure, add Hydralisks as the ranged core, then morph a meaningful portion into LurkerMP once the economy supports the gas cost. Research LurkerRange and supporting missile/armor upgrades, expand behind positional pressure, and avoid starving Drone and Overlord production while morphing the late-game army.
