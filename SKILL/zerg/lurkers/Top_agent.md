# Summary

Open with Overlord and Drones, then establish the natural Hatchery and
SpawningPool before committing to the gas-heavy route.  Do not open two
Extractors on the starting 12-14 Drones: take the first around 15-16 Drones and
the second after the natural begins mining.  Build a stable two-base
Roach/Hydralisk economy with enough copies of Extractor before advancing
through Lair, HydraliskDen, and LurkerDenMP.  LurkerDenMP
requires Lair plus HydraliskDen; it does not require InfestationPit, so do not
delay the first den or LurkerMP morphs for Hive technology.  Use Roaches to
absorb early pressure, add Hydralisks as the ranged core, then morph the first
four to six LurkerMP as soon as the den and gas are ready.

After the first Lurkers exist, add InfestationPit and Hive when the economy can
support them, because LurkerRange is a Hive-tier upgrade.  Continue supporting
missile/armor upgrades, expand behind positional pressure, and avoid starving
Drone and Overlord production while morphing the gas-heavy army.  When minerals
exceed 1000, spend them on Roaches, Drones up to the intended saturation, Queens,
Overlords, or an additional Hatchery while preserving gas for Hydralisk-to-
Lurker morphs.
