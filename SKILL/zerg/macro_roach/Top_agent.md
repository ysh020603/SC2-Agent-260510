# Summary

Play a measured Roach/Ravager macro game: secure a safe natural Hatchery, keep
Drone and Overlord production steady, then add SpawningPool, singular copies of
Extractor as needed, RoachWarren, and Lair.  Produce enough Roaches to survive
early pressure, research GlialReconstitution plus missile and ground-armor
upgrades, and morph some Ravagers when their range and Corrosive Bile are useful.

Treat the observation's worker count and ideal-worker count as authoritative.
Do not describe an economy as saturated while the current worker count is below
roughly 75% of the displayed ideal.  Normally saturate two mineral lines before
taking a fourth Hatchery; on three to four mining bases, aim for roughly 55-70
Drones before adding more bases or luxury Hive/air technology.  A macro Hatchery
for Larva is reasonable only after the active mineral lines have enough Drones.

Convert resources and Larva into immediate production instead of banking:
when minerals exceed 800 and Larva are available, queue repeated Roach batches
(or Drones if the displayed worker saturation is still low), while preserving
Overlord headroom.  When the army already has about 12-16 Roaches, or the
observation reports a clear army advantage, stop adding unrelated technology
and reinforce the main Roach/Ravager force so the background attack controller
can press the advantage and finish exposed bases.  Hive, Spire, flyer upgrades,
Ultralisks, and other composition changes are optional responses to a concrete
enemy need, not substitutes for spending resources or closing the game.
