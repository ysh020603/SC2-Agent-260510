# Summary

Prioritize a safe natural Hatchery, steady Drone and Overlord production, then SpawningPool, Extractors, RoachWarren, and Lair. Produce enough Roaches to survive early attacks while saturating two to three bases, research GlialReconstitution plus missile and armor upgrades, and add Ravagers when their utility is valuable. The long-term plan is a durable, continuously reinforced Roach/Ravager army supported by expanding Larva production and a strong mineral economy.
