# Summary

Follow a robust Roach/Hydralisk macro plan with early Hatchery, SpawningPool, Queens, Extractors, RoachWarren, and disciplined Drone and Overlord production. Secure Lair and HydraliskDen only after the economy can support them, produce Roaches for safety, then add Hydralisks and basic missile/armor upgrades. Favor simple expansion, saturation, and continuous army production over fragile timing windows, attacking only after a substantial mixed force has assembled.
