# Summary

Develop a two-base Roach economy with SpawningPool, RoachWarren, Extractors, and timely Overlords, then advance to Lair. Research Burrow, TunnelingClaws, and GlialReconstitution while maintaining enough Roaches to defend; add Ravagers when corrosive bile is useful. Once the upgrades complete, use burrow mobility and regeneration to preserve the army, pressure multiple approaches, and scale into missile and armor upgrades rather than taking wasteful frontal fights.
