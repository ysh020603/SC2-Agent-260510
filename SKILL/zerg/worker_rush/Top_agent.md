# Summary

Keep the opening extremely compact: produce only the minimum additional Drones and Overlord needed to avoid an immediate supply block, then use the existing worker count for early pressure while preparing a fast SpawningPool and Zergling follow-up. Do not spend on unnecessary gas or technology before the rush resolves. If worker damage is insufficient, preserve surviving Drones, restore mineral income, add a natural Hatchery, and transition into normal Zergling production instead of feeding workers indefinitely.
