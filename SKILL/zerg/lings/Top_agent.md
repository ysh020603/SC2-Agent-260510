# Summary

Open with early Drones into SpawningPool, take one Extractor for zerglingmovementspeed, and establish a natural Hatchery with one Queen per active base. Keep Overlords at least one production cycle ahead of the supply cap. Saturate mineral lines toward roughly sixteen Drones each, but interrupt Drones for defensive Zerglings when an early attack is visible.

Produce Zerglings in repeated large batches and attack only after speed or when a clearly exposed target appears. Use the mobility to surround isolated units, deny expansions, and reinforce continuously instead of fighting tanks, mines, or other splash damage head-on. Add at most two EvolutionChambers for melee and armor upgrades; add a BanelingNest against dense light infantry rather than continuing pure Zerglings into a hard counter.

Treat a mineral bank above roughly 600 as a Larva-production problem: add Queens and a macro Hatchery, then spend the next Larva wave immediately. Take a third base when the natural approaches saturation, replace lost Overlords before making more army, and transition to Roaches or Hydralisks if repeated Zergling waves cannot trade efficiently.
