# Summary

Play an economy-first Zerg game: keep Drone production near current saturation, add Overlords before supply blocks, expand with Hatcheries, and use Queens to improve Larva throughput. Establish SpawningPool and EvolutionChambers, then choose Roach/Hydralisk or Zergling-based production according to scouted threats while progressing through Lair and Hive. Spend excess resources on production, upgrades, and additional bases so the army can trade and remax repeatedly rather than relying on one all-in.
